Unsure / notes:

- Proverbs 7:2 p4 تا → Hc (the "and" of וֶחְיֵה rendered as purpose "so that"); p13–14 grouped "پاس دار" → H8104: the Persian repeats the verb "keep" for the second clause, which Hebrew leaves implicit from שְׁמֹר (o1).
- Proverbs 7:5 p11 زن → H5237: the noun "woman" is implied by the feminine adjective נׇכְרִיָּה. It could also be left untagged. p15–16 grouped "تملّق‌آمیز می‌گوید" → H2505 (verb "she makes smooth"). The Hm of o4 is on p10 از. p9 و has no Hebrew behind it.
- Proverbs 7:6 p7–8 grouped "از پسِ" → H1157. p12 بیرون → H8259, because "look out" is the sense of נִשְׁקָפְתִּי.
- Proverbs 7:7 grouped "در میان" and "در بین" → Hb. H995 אָבִינָה ("I discerned") has no clear Persian word; it may be absorbed in دیدم, which I left as H7200.
- Proverbs 7:9: the Persian is a loose paraphrase. شامگاهان → Hb H5399; "رنگ می‌باخت" ("was losing its colour") → H6153 (evening of the day). آنگاه که is left untagged (it could be Hb of o2). H380 (אִישׁוֹן, "the middle of" the night) has no clear rendering: I removed it from می‌باخت and left "دامن می‌گسترد" untagged.
- Proverbs 7:10: I split "لباس فاحشه" into لباس → H7897 and فاحشه → H2181, and removed the wrong tags from در and با. خباثت → H5341. آمد is left untagged (it may belong to the sense of לִקְרָאתוֹ).
- Proverbs 7:11: p1 زنی is untagged (it may stand for הִיא H1931). "تاب نمی‌آورَد" is grouped → H3808 H7931.
- Proverbs 7:12 p9 در → H681 (אֵצֶל). Hc and כָּל H3605 are not rendered.
- Proverbs 7:14 p2 نزد → H5921 (עָלַי). I left امروز as H3117 without Hd.
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
