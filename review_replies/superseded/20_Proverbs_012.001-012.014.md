Notes (unsure cases):
- Proverbs 12:4 p9 زنی: left untagged; the second half has no word for "woman" (implied by the feminine participle מְבִישָׁה), so H802 was removed.
- Proverbs 12:8 p9–p10 خوار شمرده_می‌شوند: grouped and tagged "H1961 Hl H937" for יִהְיֶה לָבוּז ("will be for contempt"); that is a loose rendering.
- Proverbs 12:9: the Persian restructures freely. "کسی به حساب نیامدن" is grouped as H7034, داشتن is tagged Hl (ל/וֹ "having"), بِه is tagged H2896 ("better"), and "خود را کسی پنداشتن" is grouped as H3513 (hithpael "honour oneself").
- Proverbs 12:10 p2–p3 در فکر: tagged H3045 (יוֹדֵעַ), with هست left untagged. آسایش is tagged H5315 (נֶפֶשׁ "life/needs").
- Proverbs 12:12 مالِ غصبی: tagged H4685 (מְצוֹד "net/prey"), which is an interpretive rendering. میوۀ خود is a translator's addition and left untagged.
- Proverbs 12:13: در is tagged Hb (the בְּ of בְּפֶשַׁע, moved to the restructured phrase). گرفتار می‌آید is an addition and left untagged.
- Proverbs 12:14: the Hm of מִפְּרִי is not tagged; از before نیکویی arguably renders the sense instead, but it is left untagged.
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
