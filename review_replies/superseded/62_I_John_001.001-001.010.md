Notes:
- I John 1:1 p29–p32 (به شما اعلام می‌کنیم): the main verb clause belongs to the Greek of 1:3 (ἀπαγγέλλομεν ... ὑμῖν). I tagged شما with G5210 and the grouped اعلام می‌کنیم with G518, both taken from a neighbouring verse (the same words also occur in 1:2: ἀπαγγέλλομεν ὑμῖν). I left آن را untagged as a resumptive pronoun.
- I John 1:1 p9 (با → G3588): I tagged it as rendering the instrumental dative article τοῖς. I'm not sure. p23 (یعنی → G4012): I kept the tag. It is a loose rendering of περί.
- I John 1:4 p9 (کامل گردد → G1510 G4137): the periphrastic ᾖ πεπληρωμένη. I'm not sure whether G1510 should be included. p4–p5 (به شما) have no Greek word behind them (ὑμῖν is absent in this text), so they stay untagged.
- I John 1:8 p3/p5 (بَری از گناهیم): an idiomatic rendering of ἁμαρτίαν οὐκ ἔχομεν. I tagged بَری with G3756 G2192 and گناهیم with G266. I'm not sure.
- I John 1:9 p23 (پاکمان می‌سازد → G2511 G2249): the suffix ـمان renders ἡμᾶς.
- I John 1:10 p15 (ندارد → G3756 G1510): "جایی ندارد" is an idiomatic rendering of οὐκ ἔστιν. I left جایی untagged. I'm not sure.
