Notes (unsure / judgement calls):
- I John 2:15 p3 آنچه tagged G3588 (τὰ, "the things"), and p6 آن tagged G2889 because it stands for "the world". The verb است (p7) was untagged: there is no verb in the original.
- I John 2:16 p2–p3 هر چه: left as هر=G3956 with چه untagged. You could argue چه renders τὸ (G3588). p15–p17 "مال و مقام" was grouped as G979 (βίος).
- I John 2:17 "تا ابد" split into تا=G1519 and ابد=G165. "به جا می‌آورد" was grouped and retagged G4160 (it had G3855). آن (p8) changed from G846 to G3588 (ὁ ποιῶν).
- I John 2:19 "رفتنشان نشان داد" is a paraphrase of ἀλλ’ ἵνα φανερωθῶσιν. Only "نشان داد" is tagged (G5319), and ἵνα is left untranslated. In "هیچ‌یک … نبودند", G3956 went on هیچ‌یک and G3756 G1510 on نبودند. That split is a judgement call.
- I John 2:20 "دارای معرفت هستید" grouped as G1492. یافته‌اید tagged G2192 (ἔχετε). آن (p5) tagged G3588 (τοῦ ἁγίου).
- I John 2:21 p28 می‌دانید (the repeated "and you know that") was kept as G1492, although the Greek only implies it. "پدید نمی‌آید" was grouped as G1510, and the negation stays on هیچ (G3956 G3756).
- I John 2:22 οὐκ (o11) is redundant and not rendered in Persian, so G3756 was removed from بودنِ. The relative که (p18) for ὁ ἀρνούμενος was left untagged.
- I John 2:25 ἐπαγγελία (G1860) has no separate Persian word: "آنچه … وعده داده_است" covers ἡ ἐπαγγελία ἣν … ἐπηγγείλατο. آنچه = G3739, and "وعده داده_است" was grouped as G1861.
- I John 2:26 and 2:28: pronoun suffixes that stand for separate Greek pronouns were tagged: گمراهتان (G4105 G5210) and آمدنش (G3952 G846).
- I John 2:27 the sentence "آن مسح، حقیقی است" restates its subject. p35 مسح kept G5545, p34 آن untagged. ὡς (o21) is not rendered. پس (p43) tagged G2532 (καὶ καθὼς), and همان‌گونه tagged G2531 only.
- I John 2:29 "آن که" changed to آن=G3588: πᾶς and καί are not rendered. The که after it was untagged (it is not ὅτι).
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
