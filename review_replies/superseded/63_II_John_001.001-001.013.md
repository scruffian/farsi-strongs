Notes (unsure cases):
- II John 1:1 p10–p11: که … ایشان را renders the relative οὓς, so the resumptive ایشان now has G3739 (it was G846). p25 که now renders the article οἱ (G3588), not G3739. p15–16: grouped دوست می‌دارم as "G1473 G25", because the verb ending carries ἐγώ.
- II John 1:3 p17 / 1:9 p2, p12, p19, p22: آن may render the article or refer back to διδαχή. I left these untagged, except هر آن کس in 1:9, which is grouped as "G3956 G3588".
- II John 1:5 p24 داشته‌ایم: the data lists εἴχαμεν as G1502 (εἴκω). It should be G2192 (ἔχω), but I used the listed G1502. Please check the source tagging.
- II John 1:6 p27 محبت: Persian makes ἐν αὐτῇ explicit as "love", so I tagged it G846. p22 او has no Greek behind it (no second αὐτοῦ), so I untagged it.
- II John 1:7 p26 ضدّمسیح‌اند tagged "G1510 G500", because the ‌اند ending renders ἐστιν.
- II John 1:8: ثمر = ἃ (G3739) and کار = εἰργάσασθε (G2038) is my reading. ἑαυτούς is not rendered.
- II John 1:9: فراتر رود is grouped as G4254. The از before تعلیم is left untagged. The καί…καί pair is rendered هم … و هم.
- II John 1:12: قلم و کاغذ ("pen and paper") reverses χάρτου καὶ μέλανος ("paper and ink"), so کاغذ is now G5489 and قلم is G3188 (it was the other way round). بیایم = γενέσθαι (G1096).
