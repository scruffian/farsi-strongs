Notes (unsure cases):
- Proverbs 28:17 p13 کسی: removed H5315. נֶפֶשׁ belongs to "blood of a soul" (rendered by خون), and کسی is the added subject of יִתְמְכוּ.
- Proverbs 28:20 p8–9 and 28:22 p3–4: grouped در پی and tagged it Hl (לְ). This is a free rendering of the preposition.
- Proverbs 28:20 p14 نخواهد_ماند: now only H3808, with H5352 kept on بی‌سزا. You could also argue for tagging both with H5352.
- Proverbs 28:21 p3/p6: moved H3808 from هیچ to نیست. به هیچ وجه is added emphasis. p9 هستند (which stands for גֶּבֶר H1397) is left untagged because it is a loose rendering.
- Proverbs 28:24 p17–19: اوباش and اراذل each render אִישׁ מַשְׁחִית (H376 H7843). I split اراذل است so that است can carry הוּא H1931.
- Proverbs 28:25 p2 حریص: H7342 H5315 (רְחַב נֶפֶשׁ). مرد is added.
- Proverbs 28:27: گرفتار نمی‌آید is grouped as H369, and H3994 is moved to لعنت. بی‌شفقت has H5956 and دارد is left untagged; the idiom "hides his eyes" is rendered freely.
- Proverbs 28:28 p8 خود: untagged, because it is the reflexive of the Niphal and not a separate word.
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
