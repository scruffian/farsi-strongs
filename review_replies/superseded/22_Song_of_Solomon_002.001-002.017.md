Notes (unsure cases):
- Song of Solomon 2:3 p17–p22: the Persian recasts "in his shade I delighted and sat" as "از نشستن در سایه‌اش لذت می‌برم"; I tagged نشستن H3427 and grouped لذت می‌برم H2530. p15 جوان (in "مردان جوان" for בָּנִים) is left untagged as an addition.
- Song of Solomon 2:7 p14: که tagged H518 because it introduces the oath's content (אִם = "that … not"). This is debatable. سیر نگشته grouped as H2654. Hs (שֶׁ) is left untagged, since تا only renders עַד.
- Song of Solomon 2:9 p3 and 2:17 p19: همچون tagged "H1819 Hl" (דָּמָה + ל "be like"). In 2:17 باش is also tagged H1819 (rule 5).
- Song of Solomon 2:12 p3: گشته tagged H7200 (נִרְאוּ "appeared", rendered "has become a garden"). This is a free rendering.
- Song of Solomon 2:13: نخستین میوه‌های grouped as H6291 (unripe/early figs). آورده tagged H2590 ("ripened"). عطرافشان شده_است grouped as "H5414 H7381" (נָתְנוּ רֵיחַ "gave fragrance"). The first לכי (o9) has no Persian equivalent.
- Song of Solomon 2:16: از آنِ (both times) grouped and tagged Hl, for possessive ל.
- Song of Solomon 2:17 p5–p7: پیش از آنکه grouped as "H5704 Hs" (עַד שֶׁ, rendered "before"). نسیمِ is left untagged. دامن بَرکِشند grouped H5127. بازگرد tagged H5437 (it was H5127).
- Song of Solomon 2:4 p13 and 2:8 p13/p19: فراز / فرازم are left untagged, following the existing "بر → H5921" pattern. Grouping "بر فراز" would also be defensible.
