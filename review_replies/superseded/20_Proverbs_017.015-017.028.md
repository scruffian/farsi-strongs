- Proverbs 17:18 p6–p8 (ضامن … می‌شود): I kept the existing tags (ضامن H6148 H6161, می‌شود H6148). לִפְנֵי is not rendered. دیگری is tagged H7453.
- Proverbs 17:19 p16–p18: I grouped "در بلندی می‌سازد" as H1361. In p20–p23 (به استقبال … می‌رود), استقبال and می‌رود both get H1245, and به is left untagged.
- Proverbs 17:20 p3–p4: I tagged کامروا H4672 H2896 and kept نخواهد_شد as H3808. Grouping these two is an alternative. I split "بلا گرفتار" into بلا (H7451) and "گرفتار می‌آید" (H5307).
- Proverbs 17:21 p3/p10 (فرزندِ): left untagged. The first one may arguably belong with H3205.
- Proverbs 17:23 p7 (عدالت): tagged H4941 only. אָרְחוֹת (H734) has no separate Persian word.
- Proverbs 17:24 p3–p6: the Persian paraphrases this. I tagged بر as H854 and grouped "چشم می‌دوزد" as H6440. سرگردان is an addition, so it is untagged.
- Proverbs 17:25: I grouped "به ستوه می‌آورد" as H3708. The two Hl prefixes are not rendered by any word. جانِ is an addition.
- Proverbs 17:26: نشاید is tagged H3808 H2896. مردمان is left untagged. גַּם is not rendered.
- Proverbs 17:28 p19 (شمرده_می‌شود): tagged H2803, the same word as می‌شمارند, because יֵחָשֵׁב covers both clauses (rule 5).
