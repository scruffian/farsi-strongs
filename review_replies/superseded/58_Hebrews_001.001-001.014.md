**Numbers taken from a neighbouring verse**
- Hebrews 1:2 p2–p11: the Persian 1:2 begins with the second half of Greek 1:1, ἐπ’ ἐσχάτου τῶν ἡμερῶν τούτων ἐλάλησεν ἡμῖν ἐν υἱῷ. The tags G1909, G3778, G2250, G2078, G1722 (به واسطۀ), G5207, G2249 and G2980 all come from 1:1 o13–o21. As a result, ما in 1:1 (p13, "our fathers") is now untagged, because ἡμῖν is used in 1:2.

**Unsure**
- **1:1 p11 با, 1:3 p13 با (G3588):** I tagged these as rendering the dative article (τοῖς πατράσιν, τῷ ῥήματι) under rule 8. If your convention is to tag only به for a dative article, drop both.
- **1:2 p15 پسری, p17 او:** these resume "son" and the relative ὃν. I treated them as added and left them untagged. Alternatively, پسری could take G5207 and او could take G3739 under rule 5.
- **1:3 p8 اوست and 1:10 توست:** I added G1510 for the است/ست copula (ὢν, εἰσιν). Remove it if you don't tag copulas carried by a suffix.
- **1:4:** the Persian swaps the two comparisons. I tagged by lexical match: فرشتگان = G32 and آنها = G846, with برتر = G1313 and والاتر = G2909. Tagging by position would reverse both pairs. I also left مقامی and the second and third از untagged.
- **1:5 p25 یا:** "و یا" stands where the Greek has καὶ πάλιν. I left یا untagged rather than treating it as a rendering of πάλιν (G3825).
- **1:7 p1 حال آنکه:** I tagged it G2532 (καί). It may instead render the μέν…δέ contrast (G3303), or be untagged.
- **1:8 p7 ای (G3588):** this is the vocative article in ὁ θεός. The ای in 1:10 has no article behind it, so it stays untagged.
- **1:12:** the Persian follows the NA text (ἑλίξεις … ὡς ἱμάτιον), but the Greek here has ἀλλάξεις and no ὡς ἱμάτιον. I tagged در هم خواهی_پیچید as G236 and left بسان and جامه‌ای untagged. I also tagged همان as G3588 G846 for ὁ αὐτός.
- **1:14 p10 به (G1223):** I tagged it for διὰ τοὺς. I also tagged وارثان as G2816, since it renders κληρονομεῖν rather than the participle μέλλοντας, which I gave to آیندۀ.
