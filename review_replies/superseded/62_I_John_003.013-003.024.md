- I John 3:15 p19: سکونت tagged G3306 (μένουσαν); ندارد keeps G2192 only, with the negation already on هیچ.
- I John 3:16 p13: the Persian "ما" renders ὑμῶν in this text (NA has ἡμῶν), so it is tagged G5210. p3–p4 "از آنجا" is tagged G1722/G3778 for ἐν τούτῳ. p11/p23 در is now untagged and راه keeps G5228. p21 خود is untagged because the second ψυχάς has no αὐτοῦ.
- I John 3:17 p15: امّا is tagged G2532 (καί read as adversative). p28 کسی now has G846 (ἐν αὐτῷ) and چنین stays untagged.
- I John 3:18: "به زبان" gets به = G3588 for the dative article τῇ, and the first به in "به راستی" gets G1722 because ἐν covers both nouns. Both are judgement calls.
- I John 3:19 p5/p7–p8: "به حق تعلق داریم" for ἐκ τῆς ἀληθείας ἐσμέν, with به = G1537 and "تعلق داریم" = G1510. This is a loose rendering. p11 خواهیم_توانست keeps G3982 (it carries the future of πείσομεν), but it could be left untagged.
- I John 3:22 p15–p17: آنچه = G3588 (τὰ) and "موجب خشنودی" = G701. The second از (p10) is untagged because it goes with اطاعت and is not ἀπό.

- I John 3:24: که (p24) for ἐκ is left untagged. The added subject او (p20) is untagged.
