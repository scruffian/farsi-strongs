Notes (unsure cases and neighbouring-verse numbers):

- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses. The original text in the file already follows the English verse division (6:13 = Hebrew 7:1, and so on), so all numbers come from each verse's own original words.
- 6:5 p7 زیرا → Hs: the causal שֶׁ. p9–10 "افسون می‌کنند" grouped → H7292. The subject pronoun הֵם (H1992) is only implied by the verb ending, so it is not tagged. p3 از → "Hm H5048": I took "از من" as rendering מִנֶּגְדִּי, and left the pronoun من untagged.
- 6:6 p2 به / p4 مانَد both → Hk: they are a split rendering of "like" (rule 5). The "ewes" (H7353) are not rendered separately, so گله‌ای → H5739 only. تازه is a translator's addition. p18 از → Hb (בָּהֶם "among them").
- 6:7 p4 روی‌بَندِ → H6777 only. The Hl of לְצַמָּתֵךְ is not rendered separately.
- 6:8 I split "ملکه توانَد" into ملکه (H4436) and "توانَد بود" (H1992, for הֵמָּה "they are"). The H1992 is debatable; "توانَد بود" could instead be left untagged.
- 6:9 p10 است → H1931 (הִיא as copula), which is debatable. The second and third הִיא are not rendered. دُختِ and آن are translator additions, so they are untagged.
- 6:10 به (p10, p14, p19) → Hk: the comparative "به زیبایی ماه" = "fair as the moon". This is plausible but not certain. درخشندگی → H1249 (בָּרָה "clear/pure") and شکوهمندی → H366. The current H366 on درخشندگی was wrong.
- 6:12 پیش (H3808) kept: "پیش از آنکه دریابم" loosely renders "I did not know". عَمیناداب → "H5971 H5081" (עַמִּי נָדִיב).
- 6:13 The NMV leaves out the second half of the Hebrew verse ("what will you see in the Shulammite…"). تا → Hc (וְנֶחֱזֶה).
- 7:2 I split "شرابِ آمیخته خالی" into "شرابِ آمیخته" (H4197) and grouped "خالی نیست" (H2637). از is untagged. The current H8326 on گندم was moved to نافِ.
- 7:3 The first שְׁנֵי ("your two breasts") is not tagged. بره‌های and مانند are translator additions.
- 7:4 روی and دارد both → H6822 ("روی به سوی … دارد" = "looking toward"), and سوی → H6440. This split is uncertain.
- 7:5 گیسوانت → "H1803 H7218" (דַלַּת רֹאשֵׁךְ). کوهِ is a translator addition.
- 7:7 همچون → "H1819 Hl" (דָּמְתָה לְ). The H2063 זֹאת is not rendered.
- 7:9 This verse needed no change. The NMV renders only the first half of the Hebrew verse; the rest (הוֹלֵךְ לְדוֹדִי …) is not in the Persian text and is not in 7:8 or 7:10 either.
- 7:11 "به روز آوریم" is grouped with شب (both H3885, non-adjacent because of را). The second بیا is untagged as a repetition.
- 7:12 "شکوفه‌های انگور" grouped → H5563 (סְמָדַר "vine blossom"). "نثارت خواهم_کرد" → "H5414 Hl" (the ت renders לָךְ's suffix).
