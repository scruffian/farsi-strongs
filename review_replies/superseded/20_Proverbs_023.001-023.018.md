Notes:
- Proverbs 23:1 p10–p11 "پیش روی" grouped as Hl H6440; p8 "در" left untagged (no Hebrew word behind it); p13–p14 "تأمل کن" grouped for the doubled בִּין (H995).
- Proverbs 23:2 p3 "شکمباره‌ای" tagged H1167 H5315 H859 ("you are a man of appetite"); the old tags for knife/throat were misplaced.
- Proverbs 23:3 p6 "زیرا" tagged Hc and p8 "آن" H1931 (וְהוּא): somewhat uncertain, since the Persian renders the clause as causal.
- Proverbs 23:5 p6–p8 "از میان رفته_است" grouped as H369; p5 "بدوزی" given H5774 (literally "make your eyes fly"); "ثروت" was added in translation, so it is untagged; H8064 is not rendered.
- Proverbs 23:7 the first half is a loose paraphrase. Only "حساب نگاه می‌دارند" (H8176) is tagged; "از آن دسته است" may reflect כְּמוֹ…כֵּן הוּא but is left untagged.
- Negated compound verbs are grouped with the negation and tagged H408 + verb (23:4, 9, 10, 13, 17). The same applies to non-adjacent مباش in 23:3 and 23:6.
- Proverbs 23:11 "علیه" = אִתָּךְ (H854); دفاع and "بر خواهد_خاست" both carry H7378. Uncertain: the Persian is a periphrasis.
- Proverbs 23:13 p1 "از" kept as Hm, but it may only govern "ادب کردن" in Persian (uncertain).
- Proverbs 23:16 p1 "چون" tagged Hb (בְּדַבֵּר "when speaking"); "اعماق وجودم" = kidneys (H3629); "به وجد می‌آید" = H5937.
- Proverbs 23:17 p13 "غیور باش" keeps H7065: the Persian repeats the elided verb, which is uncertain.
- Proverbs 23:18 "به‌یقین" given H518 (כִּי אִם "surely"), split off from "زیرا".
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
