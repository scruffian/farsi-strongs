Notes (uncertain cases):
- 8:1 p1 کاش: tagged H4310 H5414 for the idiom מִי יִתֵּן "would that". p2 مرا and p25 بر tagged Hl for לִי; p29–p30: the negation לֹא is carried by نمی‌نگریست (H3808 H936).
- 8:2 p22 بیاشامی and p31 می‌نوشاندم: tagged H8248 like p20 (the one verb אַשְׁקְךָ rendered more than once). p13 زندگی is added in translation and left untagged.
- 8:4 p12 تا: tagged H5704 Hs for עַד שֶׁ; p15–p16 and p18: the negative sense of מָה is rendered in the negative imperatives, so they are tagged H4100 H5782.
- 8:5: the second half of the Hebrew verse (o9–o17, from "under the apple tree" on) has no Persian rendering in this verse, so those numbers are not used.
- 8:6 p36–p38 شعله‌های سرکشِ آتش for שַׁלְהֶבֶתְיָה: tagged H7957 / H3050 (سرکشِ for the intensive "Yah") / H7957. Uncertain.
- 8:7 p18–p20 grouped as دار و ندار (H1952); p21 خویش, which may render בֵּיתוֹ (H1004), is left untagged. p23 به is tagged Hb for בָּ.
- 8:8 p2 را and 8:11 p2 را: these render the possessive ל ("to us", "to Solomon"), so they are tagged Hl rather than H853. 8:8 p18 او stands for לַאֲחֹתֵנוּ and is tagged H269; p10 برنیامده is tagged H369.
- 8:9 p3 and p15 بود: tagged H1931 for the copular הִיא. In p17–p18, آن را renders עָלֶיהָ but is left untagged; the H5921 tag was removed from p19 به. p20 چوب is tagged H3871 (לוּחַ) and p21 سرو H730.
- 8:12 p3–p4 از آنِ is tagged Hs Hl (שֶׁלִּי). לְפָנַי (H6440) has no clear Persian equivalent, so it is not used. p11–p12 and p21–p22 از آن(ِ) are grouped as Hl.
- 8:14 p8 همچون is tagged H1819 Hl and p12 باش H1819 (both render וּדְמֵה … לִ); p11 بچه‌آهویی is tagged H6082 H354.
