- **Psalms 1:3 p6–p7:** I moved H6388 onto جویبار ("H6388 H4325") and left کنارۀ untagged as an added word. Another option is to tag کنارۀ H6388.
- **Psalms 1:3 p14–p16:** I grouped "آرد به بار" as H5414, the idiom "to bear (fruit)" for יִתֵּן. The old tags, آرد H5034 and بار H6529, were wrong.
- **Psalms 1:5 p9 نه:** I tagged it H3808. It renders the לֹא that is carried over from the first half of the verse.
- **Psalms 2:1 and 2:12:** I grouped "از چه سبب" (Hl H4100), "به عبث" and "به دمی" (Hk H4592) as idiomatic phrases. The other way is to tag only the content word.
- **Psalms 2:2:** יַחַד (H3162) is not rendered by any Persian word. It may be implicit in "به مشورت می‌نشینند".
- **Psalms 2:7:** I tagged مرا as H413 because it renders אֵלַ/י ("to me"). I moved H859 from هستی to تو and H589 from the first من to the second. امروز has H3117 only, without Hd.
