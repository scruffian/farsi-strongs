Notes (unsure cases):
- Song of Solomon 3:1 p1 شبانگاهان: tagged H3915 only; the Hb of בַּלֵּילוֹת is absorbed in the adverb and left untagged.
- Song of Solomon 3:1 p14, 3:2 p29, 3:4 p14 (را after او): untagged because the object is a pronoun suffix (בִּקַּשְׁתִּיו / אֲחַזְתִּיו), no אֵת. 3:2 p8 (شهر را) also untagged: no אֵת before בָעִיר.
- Song of Solomon 3:2 p9 سراسر: left untagged; it may be part of the rendering of וַאֲסוֹבְבָה (H5437, tagged on خواهم_پیمود). The Hc of that verb has no Persian word. p12 و untagged (clause link added in translation); Hc of וּבָרְחֹבוֹת moved to p16 و. p13 در gets Hb, though it covers both בַּשְּׁוָקִים and וּבָרְחֹבוֹת.
- Song of Solomon 3:3 p2 شبگرد: changed H5892 → H5437 ("those going around"); the city (בָּעִיר) is only implied. p13 مرا could arguably carry H853 (را marking the object אֵת), but I left it untagged because it fuses with the 1cs suffix.
- Song of Solomon 3:3 p11, 3:4 p7 محبوب: "Hs H157" for שֶׁאָהֲבָה rendered by one noun.
- Song of Solomon 3:4 p6 که: tagged "H5704 Hs" as the rendering of עַד שֶׁ ("when/until"); somewhat interpretive. p4 چندان H4592 kept, but the Hk of כִּמְעַט is left untagged. p18–19 grouped as "رها نکردم" (H3808 H7503).
- Song of Solomon 3:5: "سیر نگشته" grouped (Hs H2654), "زحمت مرسانید" grouped (H518 H5782); the oath-negative אִם is rendered by the negative imperative. p14 که is left untagged. p10 آهوانِ is left without Hb (one به serves both nouns).
- Song of Solomon 3:6 p24 عطرآگین: changed H81 → H6999, as a repeat of מְקֻטֶּרֶת. p20 به gets Hm (מִכֹּל "with all"); H3605 is not rendered.
- Song of Solomon 3:7: no change. The ezafe in تختِ روانِ سلیمان renders שֶׁלִּ (Hs Hl) but has no separate word. p9 تن H1368 is kept, though doubtful.
- Song of Solomon 3:10 p4 بود: left untagged, though it loosely stands for עָשָׂה (H6213). p24 دوخته_بودند gets H7528. The Hm of מִבְּנוֹת is not rendered.
- Song of Solomon 3:11: بنگرید and ببینید are both H7200 (the Persian doubles the verb). p19 بر gets Hl for לּוֹ ("on his head"), and نهاد gets H5849 (crowned); سرش is untagged. The Hc of וּבְיוֹם has no Persian word.
