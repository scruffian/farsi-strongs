- Proverbs 19:17 p13–p18: پاداش and کارش both tagged H1576 (גְּמֻלוֹ "his deed/recompense"); پاداش might instead be read as part of the rendering of יְשַׁלֶּם. به تمامی (p16–p17) left untagged as an intensifying gloss; it arguably belongs with خواهد_داد (H7999).
- Proverbs 19:19 p12–p17: "ناگزیر از تکرار کار خود خواهی_بود" paraphrases וְעוֹד תּוֹסִף; only تکرار tagged H3254 (H5750 "again" is arguably also in it); ناگزیر untagged (its old H6066 was wrong).
- Proverbs 19:20 p1–p2: گر tagged H4616 (purpose clause rendered as "if you want"), uncertain. The token خواهی_سرانجام keeps H319 only (Hb not separately rendered).
- Proverbs 19:23 p5–p6: رهنمون می‌شود has no verb behind it and is left untagged; p14–p16 grouped as "به سر می‌برد" H3885 and شب kept H3885 (the idiom شب را به سر بردن renders יָלִין).
- Proverbs 19:25 p11: را arguably renders לְ of לְנָבוֹן (Hl); left untagged.
- Proverbs 19:26 p11, p16: split "خانه براند" (خانه is added) and "رسوایی است" (است is added).
- Proverbs 19:27 p4: token خواهی_از now Hm only (از renders מֵ; خواهی is added); H2308 belongs to بازایست. p3 اگر left untagged although it may reflect לִ of לִשְׁגוֹת.
