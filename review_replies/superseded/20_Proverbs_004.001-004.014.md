Notes (unsure cases):
- Proverbs 4:1 p1 «ای»: vocative particle added in translation, left untagged; p7 «پدر گوش» was a wrong group, split into پدر (H1) and گوش فرا~دهید (H8085). Hc of וְהַקְשִׁיבוּ is not rendered (Persian uses ؛).
- Proverbs 4:2 p2–p3 «شما را»: renders לָכֶם (Hl + suffix); را left untagged per rule 7, though it could arguably carry Hl.
- Proverbs 4:3 p7 «را» after «پدر خود»: renders לְ of לְאָבִי; left untagged (rule 7). p10 first «و» has no Hebrew vav, untagged.
- Proverbs 4:4 p1 «پدرم»: no אָב in the Hebrew (implicit subject), left untagged; p10 «به» treated as part of the verb's construction, untagged; «تا» tagged Hc for וֶחְיֵה.
- Proverbs 4:5 p18–p19 «از آنها»: از = Hm; آنها refers back to אִמְרֵי (H561) but left untagged as a pronoun.
- Proverbs 4:6 and 4:8 «که» tagged Hc as rendering consecutive וְ; Proverbs 4:8 second «که» (p14) left untagged — clause order is reversed versus כִּי תְחַבְּקֶנָּה, so H3588 not clearly rendered.
- Proverbs 4:7 p14 «بهای»: added, untagged; Hc of וּבְכָל not rendered.
- Proverbs 4:12 p1 «چون» tagged Hb (temporal בְּ + infinitive); «در راه» added, untagged; p11 «چون» tagged H518 (Hc not rendered).
- Proverbs 4:13 p16 «توست»: copula may render הִיא (H1931); left untagged.
