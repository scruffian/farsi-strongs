Notes:
- Proverbs 1:17 p5 (آشکارا): untagged; it was tagged H2600 a second time, but it seems to be a free rendering of "in the eyes of" rather than חִנָּם. p8–p9 grouped as «در برابر» → Hb. p11 (پرنده‌ای): כָל (H3605) is left untagged because the indefinite ـای only loosely gives "any".
- Proverbs 1:18 p3–p4 grouped «در کمین» → H693 (the idiom "lie in wait"). The ל of לְדָמָם (Hl) is not rendered. نهانی and انتظار می‌کشند are both tagged H6845. This is unsure: the translator may have spread the two verbs differently.
- Proverbs 1:19 سر‌انجام → H734 (paths → "fate/end"; loose). «سود نامشروع» is grouped → H1215. «آنچه به دست می‌آید» renders the implied gain, so it is left untagged (unsure).
- Proverbs 1:20 «کوی و برزن» renders חוּץ: کوی and برزن both → H2351. «بلند می‌کند» → H5414 (נתן קול "raise the voice").
- Proverbs 1:21 p2 «جاهای پرازدحام» keeps only H1993. רֹאשׁ (H7218) is not clearly rendered (unsure). سخن → H561 and می‌راند → H559 (it was H7121). «بانگ در می‌دهد» → H7121.
- Proverbs 1:22 the second «تا چند» repeats עַד מָתַי, so it is tagged H5704/H4970 again (rule 5; unsure). The Hc of וְלֵצִים is not rendered, and לָהֶם is not rendered.
- Proverbs 1:23 the Persian word after «کلمات خویش را» is «بر شما», which renders אֶתְכֶם, so بر → H853. This is unsure; the alternative is to leave it untagged. The را after روح and the را after کلمات are untagged because neither marks an אֵת object. بشنوید is an addition, and הִנֵּה is not rendered.
- Proverbs 1:24 the wrong groups «که فرا~خواندم» and «و آنگاه» are split. The Hc of וַתְּמָאֵנוּ has no Persian counterpart. The Hc of וְאֵין is not rendered. کسی keeps H369.
- Proverbs 1:26/27 «آنچه … می‌ترسید» renders פַּחַד: آنچه and می‌ترسید are both → H6343. In 1:27, «عارض شود» → H935 (o1), «چیره آید» → H935 (o7), and فرو~پیچد → H857 (a loose rendering of יֶאֱתֶה; unsure). توفان is corrected to H7584.
- Proverbs 1:28 بسیار (the intensive sense of שָׁחַר "seek early/diligently") is left untagged (unsure).
- Proverbs 1:31 میوۀ (2nd) has no Hebrew counterpart (implied), so it is untagged.
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
