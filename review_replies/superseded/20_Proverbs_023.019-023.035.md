- Proverbs 23:19 p13 راست: left untagged as a translator's addition. It could arguably also render H833 ("be straight"), which is now on مشغول دار.
- Proverbs 23:20 p7 نه: left untagged. It repeats the one אַל implicitly; tagging it H408 under rule 5 would also be defensible.
- Proverbs 23:22: split "روز پیری‌اش‌خوار" into روز + پیری‌اش‌خوار. The second token fuses پیری (H2204) and خوار (H936), so it carries both. "در روز" loosely renders כִּי (H3588) but is left untagged.
- Proverbs 23:24 p5/p6: kept شادی = H1523 and بسیار است = H1524 (infinitive absolute + finite verb). Swapping the two would be equally arguable. پسری and آن که are left untagged (they are implied by יולד).
- Proverbs 23:27: تاریک is a translator's addition (untagged). The number previously on it, H5237, now goes on "زن بیگانه".
- Proverbs 23:28: אַף (H637) and הִיא are not rendered. The H637 that was on می‌نشیند was removed.
- Proverbs 23:30: راست tagged Hl ("belongs to those who"). "تا دیروقت" tagged H309 together with دل…برنمی‌کنند. "در پی … می‌روند" = H935. Hl of לַחְקֹר put on چشیدن. These are idiomatic, so I am not fully sure of them.
- Proverbs 23:31: می‌درخشد = H5414 H5869 ("gives its eye", i.e. sparkles).
- Proverbs 23:34: بر = Hb only. It could arguably also carry H7218 ("top of"), which is otherwise untranslated.
- Proverbs 23:35: جرعه‌ای left untagged; it renders the object suffix of אֲבַקְשֶׁנּוּ. دیگر = H3254 H5750. بنوشم = H1245 (free rendering of "seek").
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
