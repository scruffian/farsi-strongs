Notes (unsure cases):
- Genesis 2:3 p15–p16 (در آن روز): "روز" renders the referent of the suffix in ב/וֹ, not a separate יוֹם; left untagged.
- Genesis 2:4 p7 (آنگاه) and p11 (هنگامی): tagged Hb for the בְּ of בְּהִבָּרְאָם / בְּיוֹם ("when"); somewhat interpretive.
- Genesis 2:5: second "هنوز" left untagged (the Hebrew has only לֹא here); "نبارانیده بود" tagged H3808 H4305 with "باران" also H4305; "کِشت کند" grouped as H5647 and "آن" tagged H127 (it stands for הָאֲדָמָה).
- Genesis 2:6 p1 (بلکه): tagged Hc for וְ/אֵד (adversative rendering).
- Genesis 2:8 p5 (به): retagged Hm for מִ/קֶּדֶם ("toward the east").
- Genesis 2:11, 2:13, 2:14 (که before the participle): tagged Hd since the relative "که" renders the article on הַסֹּבֵב / הַסּוֹבֵב / הַהֹלֵךְ; could be left untagged instead.
- Genesis 2:12: first "و" (after the comma) left untagged; the Hebrew וּ/זֲהַב conjunction stands at the start of the verse and is not rendered there.
- Genesis 2:16 p12 (می‌توانی → H398) and p19 (آزادانه → H398): kept as is for אָכֹל תֹּאכֵל; "را" (p5) for עַל left untagged per the را rule.
- Genesis 2:17 p8–p9 (زنهار نخوری): kept as is.
- Genesis 2:18 / 2:20: split "یاوری مناسب" into یاوری H5828 and مناسب Hk H5048 (כְּנֶגְדּוֹ).
- Genesis 2:19: "نامی" and "خواهد نهاد" both tagged H7121 (rule 5) for יִקְרָא; "نامی" could be seen as added. "را" before "خواند" untagged (no אֵת).
- Genesis 2:24 p1–p3 (از همین رو): از → H5921, همین → H3651, رو untagged; alternatively the idiom could carry both numbers.
