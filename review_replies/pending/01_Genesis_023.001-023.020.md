Notes:
- Genesis 23:2 p2 (او): tagged H8283, as the pronoun stands in for the name Sarah (o2).
- Genesis 23:3 p11–p12 (خطاب کرده): grouped and kept H413 on it, because the Persian renders אֶל with the construction "X را خطاب کرده"; را is left untagged (no אֵת).
- Genesis 23:4: in-translation phrases grouped ("در میان" = עִמָּכֶם; "غربت اختیار کرده" = גֵּר; "مقیم گشته‌ام" = תּוֹשָׁב; "قطعه زمینی" = אֲחֻזַּת). "به جهت" is left untagged because o7/o8 have no ל.
- Genesis 23:5, 23:10, 23:14: H559 (לֵאמֹר) has no Persian word (only the colon), so it is dropped from the "پاسخ دادند/داد" group.
- Genesis 23:6 p27 (هیچ‌یک): kept H376 H3808; the negation is also rendered in "دریغ نخواهیم داشت" (H3808 H3607). Uncertain. Hm of מִקְּבֹר may be loosely rendered by که (p37), which I left untagged.
- Genesis 23:8 p22 (تمنا دارم): tagged H8085 (שְׁמָעוּנִי, "hear me" used as a polite request). Uncertain. پس is left untagged. راضی is tagged H5315 (נַפְשְׁכֶם, "your will").
- Genesis 23:9 p9 (و) / p13–p14 (قرار دارد): the second אֲשֶׁר is not tagged. نظر is tagged H8432 (בְּתוֹכְכֶם, "in your presence"). p29–p30 "به جهت" is kept as is.
- Genesis 23:10 p9 (او): tagged H6085 (the second עֶפְרוֹן). H2850 (הַחִתִּי) is not rendered, so I removed it from حیتّیان. The Hl of לְכֹל has no Persian equivalent, so به (to the gate) is untagged.
- Genesis 23:13 p10–p11 (تمنا دارم): grouped as "H389 H3863" for אַךְ … לוּ. Uncertain: it could also cover H518.
- Genesis 23:15: این is tagged H1931 (הִוא). بهای and است are added. ولی is untagged.
- Genesis 23:16: the entry "رایج نزد" is split into رایج (H5674) and نزد (Hl). "بر حسب وزن" is added in translation.
- Genesis 23:17/18: Persian 23:17–18 is one sentence. مالکیت in 23:17 keeps H6965, and "واگذار شد" in 23:18 is tagged H4736. Arguably مالکیت = H4736 (23:18) and واگذار شد = H6965 (23:17), but I did not move numbers across verses. In 23:18 the Hb of בְּכֹל has no Persian word.
- Genesis 23:20: swapped the tags so that مالکیت = H272 (אֲחֻזַּת) and "واگذار شد" = H6965 (וַיָּקָם). Uncertain. "به جهت" (Hl) is kept.
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
