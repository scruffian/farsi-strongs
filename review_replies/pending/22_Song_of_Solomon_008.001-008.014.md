Notes (unsure cases):
- 8:1 p1 کاش: tagged H4310 H5414 for the idiom מִי יִתֵּן "would that"; می‌بودی left untagged. p2 مرا → Hl (לִי "to me"). The negation is folded into نمی‌نگریست (H3808 H936); کسی is the implied subject and is untagged; بر → Hl (the second לִי).
- 8:2 p22 بیاشامی and p31 می‌نوشاندم: the Persian spreads one verb, אַשְׁקְךָ (H8248), over both clauses, so all three verb forms are tagged H8248.
- 8:4 p15–p18: מַה is used here as a prohibitive "do not", so H4100 is joined to H5782 on both negated verbs. p12 تا → H5704 Hs (עַד שֶׁ).
- 8:5: the Persian verse stops after "دلداده‌اش؟". The rest of the Hebrew verse (o9–o17, the apple tree, awakening and the mother) has no Persian here.
- 8:6 p36–p38 "شعله‌های سرکشِ آتش" for שַׁלְהֶבֶתְ יָה: tagged شعله‌های H7957 and سرکشِ H3050 (the intensifying "-yah"); the second آتش is left untagged. Unsure.
- 8:7 p18–p20 grouped "دار و ندار" → H1952; خویش untagged although it loosely stands for בֵּיתוֹ (H1004). In "به پای عشق", به → Hb. The two "can't" entries carry the negation: "نتواند کرد" → H3808 H3201 and "فرو~نتواند نشانید" → H3808 H7857.
- 8:8 p10 برنیامده → H369 (אֵין "none"), a free rendering. او (p18) stands for "our sister" and is left untagged. ما را / سلیمان را (8:11): را renders the ל of possession, not אֵת, so it is untagged under rule 7.
- 8:9 بود (p3, p15) → H1931 (הִיא as subject/copula).
- 8:12 "از آنِ" (p3–p4) → Hs Hl for שֶׁלִּי. לְפָנַי (H6440) is not rendered, so that tag was removed.
- 8:13 بگذار → H8085, because it carries the causative of הַשְׁמִיעִינִי.
- 8:14 همچون kept as H1819 together with باش (H1819); the Hl prefixes are not tagged separately.
