- Numerals (2:58, 2:60, 2:64–2:69): kept the existing convention of tagging digit tokens with the number words only, without the Hc of "and two" etc.
- Ezra 2:59 p20 که → H518 (אִם "whether") and p25 اسرائیلند → H3478 H1992 (copula -ند for הֵם): somewhat interpretive. p11/p13 و untagged: no vav in the Hebrew place-name list.
- Ezra 2:60 p10 "۶۵۲." contains the period in the same token; tags kept as they were.
- Ezra 2:61 p32 خاندان untagged (it renders the suffix of שְׁמָם), H7121 moved to p36 نهاد (וַיִּקָּרֵא rendered idiomatically).
- Ezra 2:62 نداشتن / لازم untagged: part of the added explanatory phrase "به سبب نداشتن شرایط لازم".
- Ezra 2:63 p8 دارای → Hl (לְאוּרִים) and p12 برنخیزد → H5975: interpretive but likely; خوراک untagged (added word).
- Ezra 2:64 "بر روی هم" grouped as Hk H259 (כְּאֶחָד "altogether"); כָּל left unrendered.
- Ezra 2:65 p20 داشتند → Hl (וְלָהֶם "and they had"); زن → H7891 (the feminine participle מְשֹׁרְרוֹת).
- Ezra 2:68 چون → Hb (בְּבוֹאָם); התנדבו rendered by "هدایای اختیاری ... تقدیم کردند", both groups tagged H5068.
- Ezra 2:69 Hl moved from برای to به (לְאוֹצַר "to the treasury").
- Ezra 2:70 "قرار گزیدند" grouped and tagged H3427: the Hebrew second clause has no verb of its own and shares וַיֵּשְׁבוּ.
