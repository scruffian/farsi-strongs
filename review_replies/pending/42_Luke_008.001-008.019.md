- Luke 8:1 p1–p3 (پس از آن): left as is; the phrase as a whole renders Καὶ ἐγένετο ἐν τῷ καθεξῆς.
- Luke 8:4: مردم tagged G3588 (substantival τῶν … ἐπιπορευομένων); بسیاری (p4) untagged, since πολλοῦ goes with the crowd (انبوه); the context is uncertain.
- Luke 8:12: آن (p18) tagged G3056 (τὸν λόγον); I kept کلام (p10) as G3056 even though in Greek ἀκούσαντες has no object.
- Luke 8:15: پایدار می‌مانند grouped as G5281 (ἐν ὑπομονῇ); ἐν was left untagged. Uncertain.
- Luke 8:17: the negatives are distributed loosely (نگردد → G3756 G3361 G2064, covering both "known" and "come to light"); the second نیست is untagged because ἐστιν is only implied.
- Luke 8:18: به (p7) tagged G846 for the dative αὐτῷ; uncertain.
- Luke 8:19: تا او را ببینند has no Greek in 8:19 (it may echo ἰδεῖν in 8:20); I left it untagged and used no neighbouring-verse numbers.
