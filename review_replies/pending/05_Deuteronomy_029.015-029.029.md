Notes (unsure cases; no numbers were taken from neighbouring verses):

- Deuteronomy 29:15: no change. "هر کس" (p3) and "هر کس که" (p19) group که differently; left as is.
- Deuteronomy 29:16: p5 که and p7 چگونه are both tagged H834 for the first אֲשֶׁר. I left that, though که could equally be untagged. "سفر کرده، گذشتیم" condenses the two עָבַר verbs, so both are tagged H5674.
- Deuteronomy 29:17: "بی‌ارزش" is the translator's gloss on גִּלֻּלִים, so I grouped it with بتهای. "این قومها" renders the 3mp suffix and stays untagged.
- Deuteronomy 29:18: I split "باشید مبادا". "اکنون به هوش باشید" is untagged as added, and مبادا is H6435. "میوۀ … بار آورد" both render פֹּרֶה (H6509).
- Deuteronomy 29:19: چون is tagged Hb (בְּשָׁמְעוֹ "when he hears"). "خواهم بود" is tagged H1961 (יִהְיֶה). The second را has no אֵת, so it is untagged. "کسی که" is left untagged for וְהָיָה.
- Deuteronomy 29:20: the verb יֶעְשַׁן is rendered by "شعله‌ور خواهد شد", so that group is H6225 and آتش is untagged as added. او (p13) is tagged H376 H1931 for "that man", which is a judgement call. "بر سر او خواهد آمد" renders וְרָבְצָה בּוֹ: بر is Hb and "خواهد آمد" is H7257. The first را is untagged because the Hebrew has לוֹ, not אֵת.
- Deuteronomy 29:21: "بنا بر" is tagged Hk. "گرفتار آمدن به" is untagged as an expansion of לְרָעָה.
- Deuteronomy 29:22: וְאָמַר ("and will say") has no Persian rendering. "خواهند آمد" is tagged H6965 (יָקוּמוּ). "پس از" is kept as پس H310, از Hm.
- Deuteronomy 29:23: the initial و is untagged because the Hebrew verse starts without a vav; it continues the sentence of v.22. "در آن" is tagged Hb from בָהּ, which grammatically goes with יַעֲלֶה. The Persian puts it before all three clauses, so this is uncertain. "نه چیزی" is left grouped as H3808.
- Deuteronomy 29:24: "چرا" is tagged H5921 H4100 for עַל מֶה. با is tagged Hl (לָאָרֶץ). "از چه سبب" for the second מֶה is tagged only on چه.
- Deuteronomy 29:25: "از آن رو که" is kept as رو H5921, که H834; it could instead be one group, H5921 H834.
- Deuteronomy 29:26: "در برابر" is grouped as Hl (לָהֶם). The negation in "نساخته بود" goes with نصیب, so it is tagged H3808 H2505.
- Deuteronomy 29:27: "از این رو" is grouped as Hc, rendering the וַ of וַיִּחַר. "نازل فرمود" is tagged H935, and the Hl of לְהָבִיא is left unrendered.
- Deuteronomy 29:28: "چنانکه امروز چنین است". I kept امروز H3117 H2088 and چنین H2088. چنین is a loose rendering of הַזֶּה, so this is uncertain.
- Deuteronomy 29:29: "از آنِ" is treated with از as Hl and آنِ untagged, in both places. The second تا renders the Hl of לַעֲשׂוֹת.
