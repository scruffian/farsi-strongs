- Matthew 5:3–11 خوشا به حال: kept the existing convention (خوشا and حال both G3107, به untagged); grouping "خوشا به حال" as one G3107 entry would also be defensible.
- Matthew 5:2 p5–p6: grouped "آغاز کرد" as G455 (free rendering of "opening his mouth"); unsure, it could also be left untagged.
- Matthew 5:4–5: Persian follows the WHNU order (mourners in 5:4, meek in 5:5); used the variant-reading numbers.
- Matthew 5:11 p5 شما: tagged G1510 (renders the 2nd-person ἐστε of "blessed are you"; there is no ὑμεῖς there); unsure, could be left untagged.
- Matthew 5:13 p15 چگونه: tagged G1722 G5101 for ἐν τίνι; p22–p24 به کاری نمی‌آید split as G1519 / G3762 / G2480 (the negation sits in οὐδέν); p28–p32 left گردد untagged as the auxiliary of پایمالِ … گردد.
- Matthew 5:15 p17 می‌گذارد: changed G2989 to G5087 (the elided τιθέασιν); p21 همۀ آنان given G3956 G3588 for πᾶσιν τοῖς.
- Matthew 5:17 p20–p21 آمده‌ام (after بلکه) kept G2064, although the second "come" is implied, not repeated, in the Greek.
- Matthew 5:18 p15/p17: نقطه ("jot") and همزه‌ای ("tittle") had their numbers swapped; now G2503 G1520 and G1520 G2762. p12–p13 grouped "زایل نشود" G3928 (the Persian negative in a تا-clause is idiomatic, with no οὐ behind it).
- Matthew 5:20 p6/p15: تا … نباشد renders ἐὰν μή (G1437 / G3361); the previous G3756 on نباشد belonged to the later οὐ μή. ὅτι left untagged here and in 5:22 (not rendered).
- Matthew 5:3, 5:21, 5:22, 5:24: tagged the Persian preposition (در/به/بر/با) G3588 where it renders a dative article (rule 8); unsure for در in 5:3 and بر in 5:22.
- Matthew 5:22: in the third clause به برادر خود is added in Persian (Greek just "says, Fool"), so left untagged; محاکمه در حضور in the second clause is also added; شوراست given G1510 G4892.
