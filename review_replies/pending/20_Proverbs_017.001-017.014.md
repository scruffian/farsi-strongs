Notes (unsure cases; no numbers were taken from neighbouring verses):
- Proverbs 17:1 p3 در → Hb: Persian "در آرامش" renders "with quietness in it" (וְשַׁלְוָה בָהּ); Hc is not rendered and Hb is placed on در loosely.
- Proverbs 17:2 p11 یکی kept as H8432: "همچون یکی از برادران" paraphrases "in the midst of brothers" (וּבְתוֹךְ); Hb/H8432 could arguably sit on از instead.
- Proverbs 17:5 p4 را: it effectively renders Hl of לָרָשׁ but left untagged per rule 7; p6 به (object of اهانت) has no Hl behind it, so untagged.
- Proverbs 17:8 p11 جا → H834 ("wherever" = אֶל כָּל אֲשֶׁר); p13 روی نمایند → H6437 although the Persian makes the owners, not the bribe, the subject.
- Proverbs 17:9 p17 میان kept as H6504 (part of the "میان … جدایی می‌افکند" rendering); p19 خالص may also render the sense of אַלּוּף ("intimate friend") but left untagged.
- Proverbs 17:10 p13 است: H5181 removed (the verb is rendered by اثر … بیش); p12 شلاق and p14–15 بر پشت are additions.
- Proverbs 17:12 p9 بهتر است kept as H408 (וְאַל "rather than"); p11 دیدار tagged H6298 as rendering the implied second "meeting"; p7 دزدیده‌اند shares H7909 with توله‌هایش.
- Proverbs 17:14 p8 پس → Hc and p15 پیش → Hl H6440 (וְלִפְנֵי): the Persian reorders the clauses, so Hc placement is approximate.
