- I Chronicles 6:27 p16 سموئیل: "and his son Samuel" is not in the Hebrew of this verse (supplied from 1 Sam / 6:33); H511 was wrong, left untagged (H8050 from 6:28 would be the alternative).
- I Chronicles 6:28 p5 یوئیل, p7 پسر, p8 دوّمش: the Persian reads וַשְׁנִי (H2059) as "and the second" and supplies the name Joel; H2059 moved from یوئیل to دوّمش, یوئیل and the added پسر left untagged. Doubtful whether یوئیل should keep H2059 as the name slot.
- I Chronicles 6:31 p16 به → H5921 and p17 خدمت → H3027 (idiom עַל יְדֵי "under the direction of"); somewhat interpretive.
- I Chronicles 6:32 p29 وظایف kept H5656 alongside خدمتشان (same word rendered twice); p26 بودند → H1961; split "خدمتشان انجام" and grouped "انجام می‌دادند" → H5975 (וַיַּעַמְדוּ), doubtful.
