- Deuteronomy 8:2, 8:3, 8:5, 8:14, 8:19: "را" after a pronoun that renders a Hebrew object suffix is untagged under rule 7; 8:2 p34 "را" is also untagged (no את before מצותו).
- Deuteronomy 8:3 p5, 8:16 p18: the "و" inside the doublet "خوار و زبون" (for the single verb ענה) is untagged; p12 "خوراکِ" (and 8:16 p2) untagged as added.
- Deuteronomy 8:6 p15 "از": untagged; the Hl belongs to the infinitive ליראה, and "از" is required by the Persian verb.
- Deuteronomy 8:11 p5 "باشید مبادا": split into two entries; "به هوش باشید" grouped as H8104 (the reflexive לך is not translated); "نگاه ندارید" tagged H1115 H8104.
- Deuteronomy 8:14 p10, 8:15 p3, 8:16 p1, 8:18 p12 "که": these render the Hebrew article used as a relative on a participle (Hd); I left them untagged.
- Deuteronomy 8:15 p22 "بی‌آب" tagged H369 H4325; p19–p20 "زمین خشک" grouped as H6774.
- Deuteronomy 8:18 p1 "بلکه" tagged Hc (adversative rendering of the waw).
- Deuteronomy 8:19 p26 "به‌یقین" tagged H6 (renders the infinitive absolute אבד); p8–p9 "از پیِ" grouped as H310; p19 "نمایید" (the shared light verb for عبادت/سجده) left untagged.
