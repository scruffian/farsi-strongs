Notes (unsure cases; no numbers were taken from neighbouring verses):
- 5:1 p5 همان: may render the article ὁ before Χριστός (G3588); left untagged.
- 5:2 p13 / 5:3 p7 از: tagged with the verb (G4160 / G5083) as part of the idiom «از ... اطاعت کردن», following the existing 5:3 tagging.
- 5:5 p14 خداست, 5:6 p1 اوست, 5:11 p19 اوست, 5:20 p33 اوست: copula suffix tagged G1510 together with the noun/pronoun.
- 5:8 p8 این: may render οἱ (G3588) in «οἱ τρεῖς»; left untagged.
- 5:9 p17 که: could be read as the second ὅτι (G3754); left untagged since it works as a relative here.
- 5:10 p19 شهادت: the Greek has only «τῷ θεῷ»; شهادت looks like a translator's addition, so its G3141 was removed.
- 5:15 p17 آنچه: given G155 G3739 since τὰ αἰτήματα ἃ is rendered only by آنچه; p14-15 «اطمینان داریم» treated as rendering the second οἴδαμεν (G1492).
- 5:16 p21-25 «این را دربارۀ کسی می‌گویم»: translator's expansion for τοῖς ἁμαρτάνουσιν; left untagged (کسی could arguably take G3588). p16 به (for dative αὐτῷ) untagged.
- 5:18 p10-13 «در گناه زندگی نمی‌کند»: گناه → G264, «زندگی نمی‌کند» → G3756; p23 دست / p28 نمی‌رسد tagged as parts of the idiom for ἅπτεται (G680).
- 5:20 p20 او: renders τῷ ἀληθινῷ together with «که حق است»; left untagged.
