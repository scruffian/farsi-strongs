- Genesis 7:3 p1–p2 (و نیز): only نیز tagged H1571; "و نیز" together could render גַּם. Left unchanged.
- Genesis 7:4 p3–p4 (پس از): پس kept as H5750 and از tagged Hl (לְיָמִים). It is a loose match for "after seven more days".
- Genesis 7:6 p5 (که): tagged Hc for the circumstantial וְ of וְהַמַּבּוּל, which is a likely but interpretive rendering.
- Genesis 7:7 p17–p19 (در امان باشند): امان kept as H6440 (מִפְּנֵי). It is a paraphrase, so the match is loose.
- Genesis 7:13 p6 (و): the first و is kept as Hc. p7 (پسرانش) is "H1121 H5146" because ش stands for נֹחַ (o10). This is debatable: it could be H1121 only.
- Genesis 7:16 p11 (نوح): changed from the wrong H1320 to H853 (אֹתוֹ, the object pronoun rendered by a name). It could also be left untagged. p2–p3 (آنهایی که) and p21 (بودند) are left untagged: the Persian restructures הַבָּאִים … בָּאוּ.
