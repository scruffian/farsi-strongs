Notes:
- Exodus 30:20 p1/p15: وقتی and آنگاه (که) tagged Hb for the temporal infinitives בְּבֹאָם / בְגִשְׁתָּם; p11 تا tagged Hc for וְלֹא ("so that not").
- Exodus 30:21 p8 تا tagged Hc (וְלֹא); هارون renders the suffix of ל/וֹ and is left untagged; خواهد بود tagged H1961 (וְהָיְתָה).
- Exodus 30:23: مثقال (shekel) is supplied in translation and left untagged; تهیه کن grouped as קַח (H3947); the و before دارچین/نیشکر are not present, so the Hc of those words is untranslated.
- Exodus 30:25: آمیختن → H4842 and ادویۀ معطر (grouped) → H7545 is my best reading of רֹקַח מִרְקַחַת; could arguably be the other way round.
- Exodus 30:26 p4: را marks روغن (rendering ב/וֹ), not the object of אֶת, so untagged. Unsure.
- Exodus 30:28: به کار گیر is the translator's verb carried from 30:26 (וּמָשַׁחְתָּ), left untagged.
- Exodus 30:32: برای kept as H5921 (loose); به کار نبرید grouped with H3808 only (the verb יִיסָךְ is rendered by تدهین); روغن دیگری left untagged (paraphrase of כָּמֹהוּ, unsure); این → H1931; بر (شماست) → Hl for לָכֶם.
- Exodus 30:33: کاهن نیست is a paraphrase of זָר; only شخصی tagged H2114. آن را (מִמֶּנּוּ) left untagged.
- Exodus 30:34: ادویۀ خوشبو grouped → H5561 (the second סַמִּים is untranslated); صَمْغِ کاج grouped → H5198 (stacte).
- Exodus 30:35: خوشبو left untagged; آمیختن kept as H7545 (רֹקַח); unsure which of the two renders רֹקַח.
- Exodus 30:36: به شکل گَرد درآور grouped → H1854; جایی → H8033; با → Hl (לְךָ); repeated بخور left untagged (no H7004 in this verse).
- Exodus 30:38: repeated بخور untagged (H7004 not in the verse; previous H7306 was wrong).
