- I John 1:1 p30-p32: the Persian brings forward the main verb of the sentence ("آن را به شما اعلام می‌کنیم"), which in the Greek is ἀπαγγέλλομεν ὑμῖν (in 1:2 and again in 1:3). Numbers taken from the neighbouring verse 1:2: شما → G5210, اعلام می‌کنیم → G518. Arguably a translator's restatement (1:2 and 1:3 render it again), so these could be left untagged.
- I John 1:1 p23 یعنی for περί ("concerning"): a loose rendering; kept G4012 but doubtful. p9/p18 با left untagged (instrumental for the dative article τοῖς / no article for χεῖρες).
- I John 1:4 p9 کامل گردد given "G1510 G4137" (periphrastic ᾖ πεπληρωμένη); p8 شادی‌مان given G5479 G2249 (the suffix renders ἡμῶν). p4-p5 به شما has no word in the main text (TR reads ὑμῖν, not listed as a variant), so left untagged.
- I John 1:8 p3-p5 "بَری از گناهیم" is an idiomatic rendering of ἁμαρτίαν οὐκ ἔχομεν: tagged بَری → G3756 G2192 and گناهیم → G266; the split of G2192 is uncertain.
- I John 1:10 p16-p17 "جایی ندارد" ("has no place") grouped for οὐκ ἔστιν → G3756 G1510; idiomatic, uncertain whether جایی should rather stay untagged.
- I John 1:9 p3 به untagged (preposition required by اعتراف کردن, not a rendering of ὁμολογέω); p23 پاکمان می‌سازد given G2511 G2249 for the -مان suffix rendering ἡμᾶς.
