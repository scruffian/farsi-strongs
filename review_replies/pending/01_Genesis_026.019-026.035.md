Notes (unsure cases; no numbers were taken from neighbouring verses):

- Genesis 26:19 p6–p7 (چاه زدند): the Persian says "dug a well" and then "found running water", leaving out "well" in the second clause; چاه kept as H875 although it may belong to the compound verb "چاه زدن" (H2658).
- Genesis 26:20 p14–p15 (از آنِ): grouped and tagged Hl as the rendering of לָ/נוּ ("ours"); p12 این kept as Hd, which is borderline.
- Genesis 26:22 p11 (دیگر "no longer"): untagged, since it is added and does not render H312 or H3808; the negation now sits on "مجادله نکردند" (H3808 H7378).
- Genesis 26:26 p4–p5 (همراه با): grouped and tagged Hc for וַ/אֲחֻזַּת; this is uncertain and could be left untagged. H1980 moved to آمد.
- Genesis 26:27 p12–p13 (حال آنکه): tagged Hc for וְ/אַתֶּם (adversative "whereas"); אַתֶּם (H859) has no separate Persian word.
- Genesis 26:28 p10 (توست): tagged H1961 for است rendering הָיָה; p20 باشد tagged H1961 for תְּהִי. נָא (H4994) is not rendered.
- Genesis 26:29 p1 (تا): tagged H518 for the oath particle אִם; "روا نداری" renders תַּעֲשֵׂה, and the negative comes from the oath idiom, so it has no H3808. p10 ضرری is untagged as part of the paraphrase of נָגַע (it could take H5060). In "جز به نیکی رفتار نکردیم" the negative is idiomatic for רַק.
- Genesis 26:31 p1 (بامدادان) = בַ/בֹּקֶר (Hb H1242), and "صبح زود برخاسته" = וַ/יַּשְׁכִּימוּ (H7925).
- Genesis 26:33 p8 (و): left untagged, though it may loosely render עַל כֵּן (H5921 H3651).
- Genesis 26:35 p11–p12 (تلخ کردند): grouped as H1961 H4786 ("were a bitterness"); زندگی for רוּחַ (H7307) kept, though it is loose.
