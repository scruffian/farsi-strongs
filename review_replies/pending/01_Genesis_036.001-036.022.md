Notes (uncertain cases; no numbers were taken from neighbouring verses):
- Genesis 36:6 p13 "با": tagged Hc, since it stands where the וְ of וְ/אֶת מִקְנֵהוּ is. p31 "دور": tagged H6440 as the rendering of מִ/פְּנֵי ("away from"), with از kept as Hm. p27 "برگرفت": dropped H3212 (that is رفت).
- Genesis 36:7 p19 "کفاف" and p22 "نمی‌داد": the idiom "کفاف ... نمی‌داد" renders לֹא יָכְלָה ... לָשֵׂאת. I tagged both parts with the verb number, and the negated part also with H3808 and H3201. p15 "زیرا": kept as H3588, although the Persian moves the conjunction to the second clause, where Hebrew has וְ. p6 "بود": tagged H1961.
- Genesis 36:12 p10 "داشت": "Timna was a concubine to Eliphaz" becomes "Eliphaz had", so داشت gets H1961 Hl, and "به" (p7, part of "به نام") is now untagged. p11 "که": tagged Hc for the וַ of וַתֵּלֶד.
- Genesis 36:13 p11 "بودند" and 36:14 "اینانند": left as they were. Hebrew הָיוּ is in the other clause.
- Genesis 36:15: "سران طایفه‌های" grouped as H441, and "بنی‌عیسو" tagged H1121 H6215. The second "اینانند" (p6) is untagged because Hebrew has only one אֵלֶּה. H441 removed from the names because "سران طوایف" already renders it.
- Genesis 36:20 p7 "همان": left untagged. It might render the article of הָ/אָרֶץ.
