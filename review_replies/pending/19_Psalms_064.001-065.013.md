- Psalms 64:1 p3 چون tagged Hb (בְּשִׂיחִי rendered as "when I lament"); somewhat interpretive.
- Psalms 64:5 p1 خویشتن / p3 به untagged: H2388 already on مسلح می‌کنند; به has no Hebrew preposition behind it (לָמוֹ is rendered by خویشتن را).
- Psalms 64:6 کاملی → H8552 (תַּמְנוּ), اندیشیدیم → H2664 (מְחֻפָּשׂ); the Persian paraphrases freely, so this split is uncertain.
- Psalms 64:8 p18 بر kept as Hb, but בָם really goes with רֹאֵה ("who see them"); unsure. The Persian paraphrase of ויכשילוהו (بر خواهد گردانید / نابودشان خواهد کرد) is loose.
- Psalms 65:8 مغرب/مشرق render "outgoings of evening/morning", so both get H4161 plus H6153/H1242 respectively; وا می‌داری also tagged H7442 along with بانگ شادی.
- Psalms 65:11 نیز tagged Hc (וּ/מַעְגָּלֶיךָ); جای چرخ ارابه‌هایت grouped as H4570.
- Psalms 65:13 و (p15) tagged H637 (אַף "yea").
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
