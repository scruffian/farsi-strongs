Notes:
- 5:17–5:19: left unchanged; the noun carries the verb number and مکن carries H3808 (5:18 also adds H5003 on مکن). In 5:20, 5:21 and 5:32 I tagged the negative light verb "H3808 + verb" (مده, مورز, مخور, مشوید), as 5:18 does. One convention could be applied to all of them, or each compound verb could be grouped instead.
- 5:21 p30 دیگر → H834 kept but doubtful; "هیچ چیز دیگر او" freely renders כל אשר לרעך. p31 او (for לְרֵעֶךָ) is left untagged.
- 5:22 p26 چیزی دیگر is now untagged (added in translation); the negated יָסָף is on نیفزود.
- 5:25 چرا tagged "Hl H4100" (the whole word לָמָּה). In "بیش از این", עוד (H5750) has no clear Persian word, so این is untagged and بیش stays H3254.
- 5:28 p29 آنچه kept as H834; it may also render כָּל (H3605).
- 5:29 کاش tagged "H4310 H5414" for the idiom מִי יִתֵּן. داشتند is tagged H1961, which is a loose rendering. "به جای می‌آوردند" is grouped as H8104 and its Hl is not tagged.
- 5:31 تا (p8, p26) tagged Hc for the waw of וַאֲדַבְּרָה / וְעָשׂוּ. This is doubtful.
- 5:32 "به شما" renders אֶתְכֶם, so به is tagged H853. This is doubtful because it is not را.
- 5:33 "به تصرف خواهید آورد" is grouped as one compound verb (H3423).
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
