Notes:
- Deuteronomy 12:1 p9 (تا): untagged; "تا زمانی که" loosely renders כָּל הַיָּמִים ("all the days"), so H3605 would also be defensible. p24–p25 grouped as "به جهت" (Hl of לְרִשְׁתָּהּ); p27 به tagged Hl for לְךָ.
- Deuteronomy 12:5 p3 (را): tagged H413 because אֶל marks the object of תִדְרְשׁוּ; the verse's only אֵת belongs to שְׁמוֹ (p18). Not certain. p23 آن / p24 را: untagged (they paraphrase לְשִׁכְנוֹ, and there is no second אֵת).
- Deuteronomy 12:6 p13–p14: grouped "قربانیهای نذری" → H5088, with قربانیهای as the head noun. It could also be left untagged as an added word.
- Deuteronomy 12:7 p17 دسترَنج: given H4916 H3027 because it renders מִשְׁלַח יֶדְכֶם. p16 از: tagged Hb.
- Deuteronomy 12:10 p3 از: untagged (it is a Persian preposition with عبور کرده; אֶת is not rendered as را). p23 را: untagged because the Hebrew has לָכֶם, not אֵת. p33 تا: tagged Hc for וִישַׁבְתֶּם.
- Deuteronomy 12:11 p12 به (به مکانی): untagged; Hb belongs to בּוֹ, which is not rendered. p5 چیزها: untagged.
- Deuteronomy 12:12 p27–p31 (و ایشان را چون شما … نیست): left unchanged, but not sure. چون may render אִתְּכֶם ("like you", H854) rather than כִּי (H3588).
- Deuteronomy 12:15 p7/p10: אַוַּת (H185) moved from اندازه to می‌خواهد. p36 می‌توانند and p43 گوشت: untagged because they are added in translation.
