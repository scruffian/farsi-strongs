Notes (unsure cases):

- Numbers 31:1 p3 / 31:3 p4: را tagged H413 because "X را خطاب کرده" renders דִּבֶּר אֶל X; it could also be left untagged.
- Numbers 31:2 p5: از given Hm H854 for מֵאֵת. "پس از" grouped as H310.
- Numbers 31:3: تا → Hc and برآیند → H1961 (וְיִהְיוּ עַל); و (p23) → Hl and بستانند → H5414 for לָתֵת נִקְמַת. This is a free rendering, so these tags are less certain.
- Numbers 31:6: تن was tagged H6635, which is wrong. "هزار تن" is now grouped as H505. "به جهت" left untagged because the Hl it had is not in הַתְּרוּעָה. همراه kept as Hc only (וְאֶת).
- Numbers 31:7 p14: را untagged because there is no אֵת before כָּל זָכָר. به (p5) before موسی renders אֶת and is left untagged.
- Numbers 31:8: "همراه با" grouped as H5921 (עַל חַלְלֵיהֶם) and دیگر untagged. "دم شمشیر گذراندند" split into "دم شمشیر" H2719 and گذراندند H2026 (the second הָרְגוּ).
- Numbers 31:9: "به اسارت گرفتند" and "به غنیمت بردند" grouped as compound verbs.
- Numbers 31:11 p16: بردند → H3947 (וַיִּקְחוּ). به is untagged because it does not render the Hb of בָּאָדָם.
- Numbers 31:12 p19: واقع is left untagged. It might reflect אֲשֶׁר, but that word sits in a different place.
- Numbers 31:16: Hard to map. هم‌اینان → H2007, "سبب شدند" → H1961, بودند untagged. در → H5921 only (עַל דְּבַר פְּעוֹר). "گرفتار آمدند" is left untagged (it may loosely render וַתְּהִי).
- Numbers 31:17: "از میان" grouped as Hb (בַּטָּף). نیز is untagged because the Persian omits the second הֲרֹגוּ. با/وی/"همبستر شده‌اند" kept as before (وی H2145).
- Numbers 31:18: کسی → H2145 (rule 5, along with مردی). نشده‌اند and با are untagged. The negation in نشده‌اند could arguably carry H3808.
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
