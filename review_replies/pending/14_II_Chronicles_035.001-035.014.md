- II Chronicles 35:4: swapped prepositions to match the Persian order: مطابق → Hb (בִּכְתָב), "بر حسبِ" → Hl (לְבֵית), در → Hk (כְּמַחְלְקוֹתֵיכֶם).
- II Chronicles 35:5: second "بر حسبِ" left untagged (it renders the construct relation of וַחֲלֻקַּת, no preposition); its گروههای → H2515.
- II Chronicles 35:7 p31: kept همه → H3605 H428 (הַכֹּל / אֵלֶּה), somewhat uncertain.
- II Chronicles 35:8 p2: نیز left untagged (could render the ו of וְשָׂרָיו).
- II Chronicles 35:10: ترتیبات and داده شد (non-adjacent) both tagged H3559 for וַתִּכּוֹן; somewhat interpretive.
- II Chronicles 35:11 p12: به left untagged (paraphrase of מִיָּדָם; could be Hm).
- II Chronicles 35:13: به‌سرعت and رساندند both tagged H7323 (וַיָּרִיצוּ "made run / brought quickly").
- II Chronicles 35:14: بنابراین tagged Hc (וְהַלְוִיִּם), mildly uncertain.
