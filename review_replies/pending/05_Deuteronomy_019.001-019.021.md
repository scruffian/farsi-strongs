Notes (uncertain cases; no numbers were taken from neighbouring verses):

- Deuteronomy 19:1 p18–p20: وارث … شده is a split compound verb for וִירִשְׁתָּם, so both parts are tagged H3423 (rule 5).
- Deuteronomy 19:3 p21: بتواند is still tagged H5127. It may be only a modal added in translation.
- Deuteronomy 19:4 p14/p15: هرگاه is tagged H834 (the conditional אֲשֶׁר) and کسی is untagged. p22: بی‌آنکه is tagged Hc H3808 for וְהוּא לֹא. p23 با is tagged Hl for לוֹ. p27 داشته باشد is tagged H8130 as part of دشمنی داشتن (rule 5).
- Deuteronomy 19:5 p19: با before دستش is untagged. It could be read as rendering the ב of בַּגַּרְזֶן. p22 را is untagged because there is no אֵת.
- Deuteronomy 19:6 p5–p6: در شدت خشم renders כִּי יֵחַם לְבָבוֹ, and لְבָב (H3824) has no separate equivalent. خشم is left untagged. p26–p27 حال آنکه is grouped and tagged Hc. نبوده is tagged H369.
- Deuteronomy 19:9 p26: پیوسته is tagged H3605 H3117 for כָּל הַיָּמִים. p37: نیز is untagged and دیگر is tagged H5750.
- Deuteronomy 19:10 p22: نباشد is tagged H1961. The Persian negates the clause, but the Hebrew is positive (וְהָיָה … דָּמִים).
- Deuteronomy 19:11 p5: از is tagged Hl (לְרֵעֵהוּ). p13: باشد is tagged H693 as part of در کمین بودن. p23: کشنده is tagged H5315 (נֶפֶשׁ). p25: که is tagged Hc (וָמֵת). These are interpretive.
- Deuteronomy 19:15 p5: تن is untagged, since H376 belongs to کسی. It could instead be tagged H5707.
- Deuteronomy 19:16 is left unchanged. به currently has "Hl Hb", and how לַעֲנוֹת בּוֹ maps onto او را به … متهم کند is unclear.
- Deuteronomy 19:17 p2–p4: هر دو is tagged H8147 and طرفِ is tagged H376 (הָאֲנָשִׁים).
- Deuteronomy 19:19 p12: بدین‌گونه is tagged Hc (וּבִעַרְתָּ). This is interpretive.
- Deuteronomy 19:20 p8–p9: H5750 (עוֹד) is not assigned. It could go on دیگر together with H3254 or with هرگز. p17 نخواهند شد is tagged H6213 as part of مرتکب شدن.
