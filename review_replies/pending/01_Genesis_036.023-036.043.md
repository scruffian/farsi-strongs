Notes:
- Genesis 36:29–30, 36:40–43: the Persian lists names without repeating "chief" (אַלּוּף) before each one. I made the tags consistent: every such name now carries H441 plus its own number, in original order (H441 first). The existing tags had been inconsistent, sometimes leaving H441 off, putting it in the wrong order, or giving the wrong name number: 36:40 p15 عَلوَه had H8555 H441 H5933. Dropping H441 from the names altogether would also be defensible.
- Genesis 36:24 p13–14 «به هنگام» grouped → Hb (בִּ/רְעֹתוֹ); p20–22 «چشمه‌های آب گرم» grouped → H3222 (one Hebrew word). p8–9 «این همان» render הוּא; I left H1931 on همان.
- Genesis 36:30 «بر حسب», 36:40 and 36:43 «بنا بر»: grouped and tagged Hl. In 36:40 the ל/ב prefixes on מְקֹמֹתָם and שְׁמֹתָם have no separate Persian word.
- Genesis 36:31 «پیش از» grouped → Hl H6440; p14 بر → Hl (לִ/בְנֵי).
- Genesis 36:33–39 «به جای» grouped → H8478 (תַּחְתָּיו). I left «پس از مرگ» as it was (پس → Hc).
- Genesis 36:35 p10 که renders the article/participle הַ/מַּכֶּה. I left it untagged (Hd is doubtful).
- Genesis 36:39 p26 the repeated مَطرِد had H4314; I corrected it to H4308.
- Genesis 36:43 הוּא is rendered by این, not بود: H1931 moved to این. «تصرف کرده بودند» grouped → H272 (noun אֲחֻזָּתָם rendered as a verb phrase).
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
