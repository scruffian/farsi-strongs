- Leviticus 9:14 p10–p12: عַל הָעֹלָה rendered by با (H5921); بر before مذبح renders only the directional ה, so left untagged.
- Leviticus 9:15: "قربانی گناه تقدیم کرد" grouped and tagged H2398 (וַיְחַטְּאֵהוּ, "offered it as a sin offering"); به عنوان left untagged.
- Leviticus 9:17: "علاوه بر" grouped as Hm Hl H905 (מִלְּבַד); برگرفت tagged H4390 (וַיְמַלֵּא כַפּוֹ "filled his palm").
- Leviticus 9:19: the H853 of וְאֶת הַחֲלָבִים is rendered by the را at the start of Persian 9:20 (already tagged there); the Persian verse division differs here, but no number was moved between verses. - Leviticus 9:20 p7 (هارون) is not in the Hebrew of 9:20 (implied subject), so it is left untagged.
- Leviticus 9:22: split the wrongly grouped "و پس" into و (Hc) + "پس از" (Hm).
