- II Chronicles 30:19 p13 "ببخشاید" tagged H3722 taken from 30:18 (o24 יְכַפֵּר).
- II Chronicles 30:18 p23-25 "و با این‌حال": the wrong Hd was removed and the words were left untagged. They might render כִּי (o11, "yet"), but I'm unsure.
- II Chronicles 30:20 p4 / 30:21 p27 / 30:22 p32: the H853 on را was removed because the Hebrew has אֶל / לַ there, not אֵת.
- II Chronicles 30:25 p36-40 "به شادی و سرور پرداختند": both شادی and سرور are tagged H8055, and the joining و is left untagged. I'm unsure about this.
