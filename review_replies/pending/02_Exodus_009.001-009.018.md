Notes (unsure cases; no numbers were taken from neighbouring verses):

- Exodus 9:1 p21, 9:13 p28: مرا (= من + را) tagged H853, since its را marks the object עַמִּי after אֶת; the pronoun part is the suffix. The second مرا (suffix of וְיַעַבְדֻנִי) is untagged.
- Exodus 9:2 p2: از tagged Hl for the infinitive לְשַׁלֵּחַ (doubtful). p9 از kept as Hb (בָּם) though it mostly belongs to the Persian مانع از construction; p11 شوی tagged H2388 as the second part of مانع … شوی.
- Exodus 9:3: بلایی مَهیب split; مَهیب renders כָּבֵד מְאֹד. خواهد فرستاد tagged H1961 (הוֹיָה), a loose rendering.
- Exodus 9:4 p12–p14: به گونه‌ای که left untagged, though it may render the ו of וְלֹא. حیوانات untagged (added); H1121 moved to بنی‌اسرائیل.
- Exodus 9:5: این (p12) untagged; H2088 moved to چنین (הַדָּבָר הַזֶּה).
- Exodus 9:9: the two וְהָיָה are tagged on تبدیل شده and بدل خواهد گردید. پخش خواهد شد left untagged (it had H6524/H1961), and H6524 פֹּרֵחַ has no clear Persian equivalent. Doubtful.
- Exodus 9:10 p31: بدل گشت retagged H1961 (וַיְהִי) instead of H6524 (doubtful, as in 9:9).
- Exodus 9:11 p8: the Persian has فرعون where the Hebrew has מֹשֶׁה, so it is left untagged (it was H4872). آنها tagged H2748 (בַּחֲרְטֻמִּם).
- Exodus 9:14 p9: خود tagged H3820, since "upon your heart" is rendered "upon yourself" (doubtful). p8 بر kept as "H413 Hb".
- Exodus 9:15: بیاورم tagged H5221 (וָאַךְ). صفحۀ untagged, and محو شوی grouped as H3582. می‌توانستم left untagged.
- Exodus 9:16 p7–p8: grouped as به پا داشتم (H5975). بر تو (p13–p14) untagged, since it renders the suffix of הַרְאֹתְךָ.
- Exodus 9:18 p1: بنابراین left untagged, though it may loosely render הִנְנִי (H2005). The بر before مصر is untagged; בְּמִצְרַיִם is split between مصر and در (Hb).
