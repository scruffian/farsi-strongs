- Genesis 10:12 p16–17 (بنا کرد): H1129 taken from the neighbouring verse 10:11 (o6 וַיִּבֶן); the Persian moves the verb "built" to the end of v.12. Accordingly removed the wrong H1129 from آنجا in 10:11.
- Genesis 10:11 last entry و (Hc): the Hebrew "and" belongs to וְאֶת רֶסֶן in 10:12 (o1); left as Hc.
- Genesis 10:8 که (H1931): Persian renders the pronoun הוּא "he" as a relative "who"; tagged H1931, somewhat doubtful.
- Genesis 10:10 مراکز اصلی: grouped as the free rendering of רֵאשִׁית "beginning" (H7225); مراکز could alternatively be left untagged.
- Genesis 10:9 از این رو: tagged از H5921, این and رو H3651 for עַל כֵּן; could alternatively be grouped.
- Genesis 10:12 آن: tagged H1931 for הִוא ("that is the great city"), and است untagged (part of added "واقع است").
- Genesis 10:14 ایشان: renders מִשָּׁם "from there" as "from them"; tagged H8033 (moved off کسلوحیم).
