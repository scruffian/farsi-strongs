- Genesis 14:2 p1-p2 "این پادشاهان": added subject (the kings of v1), untagged. p3 به untagged; the idiom "به جنگِ ... رفتند" renders עָשׂוּ מִלְחָמָה (جنگ H4421, رفتند H6213). Fixed swapped names (Birsha H1306, Shinab H8134, Shemeber H8038).
- Genesis 14:2/14:8/14:17 "که ... باشد": both kept as H1931 (as currently tagged); in 14:3 باشد left untagged as before. Unsure which convention is wanted.
- Genesis 14:3 p13-p14 "با هم": left untagged, only "متفق شدند" grouped as H2266; could be grouped with it.
- Genesis 14:9 p17 split "اِلاسار" / "بجنگند"; بجنگند left untagged (the battle is rendered in v8 by صف‌آرایی کردند; H4421 from v8 could be argued).
- Genesis 14:10 p4 "پر" kept H875 (renders the reduplicated בֶּאֱרֹת בֶּאֱרֹת "full of pits"); doubtful. "در آنها" grouped as H8033 (שָׁמָּה).
- Genesis 14:12 p17 "بردند" tagged H3947 H3212 (took ... and went); doubtful whether H3212 should be included.
- Genesis 14:13 p11 second "اَبرام" renders וְהוּא, tagged H1931. p8 را (for לְ) left untagged.
- Genesis 14:15 interpreted "به دشمن حمله بردند" as rendering עֲלֵיהֶם (حمله بردند H5921) and "شکست داده" as וַיַּכֵּם; doubtful.
- Genesis 14:16 p14 "با" kept H853 (renders וְגַם אֶת); Hc H1571 might be better.
- Genesis 14:17 "پس از آنکه" grouped as H310; Hc of וַיֵּצֵא not rendered.
- Genesis 14:23 "اموال تو" taken as מִכָּל אֲשֶׁר לָךְ (از Hm, اموال H3605 H834); که tagged H518 (oath particle); برنگیرم tagged H3947 only, though its negation comes from the second אִם (H518); یا tagged Hc H5704 (וְעַד) - doubtful.
