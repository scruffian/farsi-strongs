Notes:
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
- Deuteronomy 31:16 p3 را: here it stands for אֶל (no אֵת for Moses), so I left it untagged under rule 7. p34 / 31:20 p47 مرا (= من + را): tagged H853, because the را marks the object where the verse has אֵת before בְּרִיתִי. p19–p20 grouped as "از پیِ" for אַחֲרֵי.
- Deuteronomy 31:17 "از آن رو" → עַל, and "فرود نیامده" → H3808 H4672 (הֲלֹא … מְצָאוּנִי). Removed H4672 from بر, which only renders the object suffix. Unsure about تلف خواهند شد (H398 only; וְהָיָה is not tagged separately).
- Deuteronomy 31:18 p21 به‌یقین: tagged H5641 for the infinitive absolute הַסְתֵּר.
- Deuteronomy 31:21 چون → Hl and "بر ضد" → Hl H6440 (לְעֵד / לְפָנָיו): somewhat unsure. "هم‌اکنون" means today (הַיּוֹם), and مقاصد renders יִצְרוֹ. The clause אֲשֶׁר הוּא עֹשֶׂה is not rendered.
- Deuteronomy 31:24 p1 چون: kept as Hk; וַיְהִי and כַּלּוֹת have no Persian word of their own. I'm unsure whether نوشت should also carry H3615.
- Deuteronomy 31:25 p1 به: removed H6680 and left it untagged. It introduces the object (אֶת הַלְוִיִּם) but is not را.
- Deuteronomy 31:27 عِصیان می‌ورزید: kept H4784; it could also take H1961 (the periphrastic הֱיִתֶם). I left هنوز as H5750 without Hb.
- Deuteronomy 31:29 p53 خداوند: left untagged because it renders the suffix of לְהַכְעִיסוֹ. "به تمامی" renders the infinitive absolute הַשְׁחֵת (H7843).
