Notes (uncertain cases; no numbers taken from neighbouring verses):

- Leviticus 18:2 p13, 18:4 p19, 18:5 p22, 18:6 p21: هستم kept as H589 (copula supplied in Persian for the verbless clause); could arguably be untagged.
- Leviticus 18:3 p21: می‌برم given H589 H935, since the subject pronoun אֲנִי is rendered only by the verb ending. The second לֹא תַעֲשׂוּ (o17–o18) has no separate Persian word apart from نه (p12, H3808).
- Leviticus 18:5 p15–p16: grouped "به واسطۀ" as Hb (בָּהֶם "by them").
- Leviticus 18:6 p2/p7: هیچ‌یک → H376 (אִישׁ אִישׁ), اَحَدی → H3605 (כָּל); p9 محارمِ → H7607 H1320 (שְׁאֵר בְּשָׂרוֹ). The و (p12) for לְ of לְגַלּוֹת left untagged.
- Leviticus 18:7–18:15: the subject pronoun הִוא is now tagged on او/آن instead of on the copula توست. In 18:7 p4 که, which loosely renders וְ of וְעֶרְוַת, is left untagged.
- Leviticus 18:9 p25: بیرون → H2351 only; the second מוֹלֶדֶת is elided in Persian.
- Leviticus 18:10 p15–p16: הֵנָּה (H2007, used as a copula) has no clear Persian word (perhaps the است in توست); left untagged.
- Leviticus 18:14 p15: split the wrongly grouped "منزلۀ عمۀ"; به منزلۀ is added in translation, and عمۀ → H1733.
