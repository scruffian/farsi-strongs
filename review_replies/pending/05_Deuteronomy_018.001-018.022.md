- Deuteronomy 18:8: free paraphrase. برابر → "Hk H2506" (כְּחֵלֶק); از (p15) untagged as it does not render עַל; "اموال خانوادگی" renders עַל הָאָבוֹת loosely.
- Deuteronomy 18:10 p19 (فالگیر): given both H7081 and H7080 for קֹסֵם קְסָמִים (the source's numbers for these two words look swapped, but I kept them in original word order as listed).

- Deuteronomy 18:16 p21 (دیگر): kept "H3254 H5750"; the Persian uses one دیگر for both "again" clauses.

- Deuteronomy 18:21 p1–p2 (ممکن است): may render וְכִי; left untagged. خود (p4) tagged H3824 for בִּלְבָבְךָ.
