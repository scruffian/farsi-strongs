- Judges 17:2: the Persian closing sentence (پس اکنون نقره‌ها را به تو بازپس می‌دهم) renders the end of 17:3 (וְעַתָּה אֲשִׁיבֶנּוּ לָךְ); numbers Hc, H6258, H7725 (and Hl) taken from Judges 17:3 o19–o21. That second نقره‌ها renders a pronoun suffix and is left untagged.
- Judges 17:2: the ketiv ואתי (H859, "you") is expressed only by the verb ending of نفرین کردی and is not tagged separately; از تو is tagged from לָךְ (Hl).
- Judges 17:5: داشت (rendering ל/וֹ "to him", i.e. possession) is left untagged; تا retagged Hc (from וַיְהִי), with باشد as H1961. Both are uncertain.
- Judges 17:8: چون سیר می‌کرد is tagged as Hl + H6213 H1870 (לַעֲשׂוֹת דַּרְכּוֹ); the grouping is uncertain.
- Judges 17:10: به خانۀ میکاه is added in translation (the Hebrew has only "the Levite went"), so it is untagged; تأمین keeps H4241 and the added verb خواهم کرد stays untagged.
- Judges 17:11: میکاه renders the suffix of ל/וֹ (untagged), and הַנַּעַר (H5288) is not rendered.
