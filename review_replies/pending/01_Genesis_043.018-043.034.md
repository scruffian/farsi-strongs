- Genesis 43:18 p2 آنها tagged H376 ("the men"); p3–p4 grouped "از اینکه" for כִּי (H3588), so p11 زیرا now carries the Hc of וַיֹּאמְרוּ (unsure: could be the other way round). p17 ما = אֲנַחְנוּ (H587), so p18 را has no אֵת behind it. p29–p30 "به اینجا" is added. p35/p37: حمله آورده = לְהִתְגֹּלֵל (H1556) and گرفتارمان کند = לְהִתְנַפֵּל (H5307), assigned by sense; this is uncertain. p43 سازد and p48 بگیرد both render לָקַחַת (H3947), and H2543 is removed from بگیرد. p23 بار was left untagged; the Hb of בַּתְּחִלָּה could also go there.
- Genesis 43:19 p4 پیشکار now has H376 H834 H5921 ("the man who was over"). p9 به is the "at" of the doorway, and p12 با renders אֵלָיו (H413).
- Genesis 43:21 p3 در = אֶל (H413), although בָּאנוּ is not explicitly rendered. p20 یافتیم tagged H2009 (וְהִנֵּה), which is unsure. p25 بازآورده‌ایم: H3027 is dropped because "in our hand" is not rendered. The same applies to Genesis 43:22 p5.
- Genesis 43:23 p6 بر = לָכֶם (Hl). p27 نقد: the wrong H4301 is removed.
- Genesis 43:24 p3 ایشان = "the men" (H376), and p14 تا carries the Hc of וַיִּרְחֲצוּ (unsure).
- Genesis 43:26 p13 نزد and "در برابر" render the two לוֹ (Hl). p24 روی is untagged; the phrase "روی بر زمین نهادند" is a paraphrase of וַיִּשְׁתַּחֲווּ … אָרְצָה.
- Genesis 43:30 "متأثر شده بود" now carries H3648 H7356. "به شدّت" is left untagged (unsure). "گریستن بیابد" is split: بیابد = H1245. "بیرون رفت" (p12–13) is treated as added.
- Genesis 43:32 "این کار" = הִוא (H1931), which is unsure.
- Genesis 43:34 p3 آنان = אֲלֵהֶם (H413). p5 از = Hm H854. p6 سفرۀ and p7 یوسف (for פָּנָיו) are left untagged; this is unsure. p16 "داده شد" could correspond to וַתֵּרֶב (H7235) but is left untagged.
