Notes:
- Proverbs 12:2 p13 (خداوند): untagged — the second "LORD" is supplied by the translator as the implied subject of יַרְשִׁיעַ; no second יְהוָה in the Hebrew. Hm of מֵ/יְהוָה is not rendered (را construction), left untagged.
- Proverbs 12:3 p11–p12 (نتوان کَند): grouped as "cannot be uprooted" = בַּל יִמּוֹט (H1077 H4131); moderately confident.
- Proverbs 12:4 p11–p14: grouped "شرمساری به بار آورد" as the rendering of מְבִישָׁה (H954). Hc tagged on اما; Hc not given to any word for the first clause.
- Proverbs 12:8 p9–p10: "خوار شمرده_می‌شوند" given H1961 Hl H937 (יִהְיֶה לָ/בוּז); p3 فراخورِ = לְ/פִי "according to" (Hl H6310).
- Proverbs 12:9: free rendering; "کسی به حساب نیامدن" = נִקְלֶה (H7034), داشتن = ל/וֹ (Hl), بِه = טוֹב, "خود را کسی پنداشتن" = מִתְכַּבֵּד (H3513). Somewhat interpretive.
- Proverbs 12:10 p2–p4: "در فکر" = יוֹדֵעַ (H3045), آسایش = נֶפֶשׁ (H5315); free rendering, unsure.
- Proverbs 12:11 p15–p18: از untagged; پی and می‌دود both H7291 (discontinuous rendering of מְרַדֵּף), باد = רֵיקִים (H7386).
- Proverbs 12:13 p8–p9 (گرفتار می‌آید): left untagged (verbless Hebrew clause; no verb behind it).
- Proverbs 12:14 p6 (از): left untagged; Hm belongs to מִ/פְּרִי, which the Persian renders as subject. Hc of וּ/גְמוּל not rendered.
