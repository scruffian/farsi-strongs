Notes:
- Numbers 2:4, 2:6, 2:8, 2:9, 2:11, 2:13, 2:15, 2:16: the digit numeral now carries every number word of the original once, in original order (the Hc inside the compound numbers is left out), and تن is untagged. Before, part of the numbers was on تن. There is no precedent for numerals written as digits, so please check this convention.
- Numbers 2:2 p14 رو: tagged H5048 (מִנֶּגֶד "opposite") for رو به. The Hm is left untagged. p5 نزد: tagged H5921 (עַל דִּגְלוֹ). p3 از is partitive, so Hm was removed. p8 و (בְּאֹתֹת) is left untagged.
- Numbers 2:3 p5 در: tagged Hl (לְצִבְאֹתָם). Numbers 2:9 and 2:16 بر حسب: grouped and tagged Hl. Both are judgement calls.
- Numbers 2:1: خطاب کرده is grouped as H1696. The H413 was dropped because "to" is implicit in را/خطاب.
- Numbers 2:17 p26 جای: tagged H3027 (יָדוֹ "his place"). نزد عَلَمِ was split into نزد (Hl) and عَلَمِ (H1714).
- Numbers 2:7: سپس is untagged because 2:7 has no ו (unlike 2:14).
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
