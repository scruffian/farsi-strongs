- All verses: "پس از" for אַחֲרֵי (5:19, 5:22, 5:26, 5:30) and "به سبب" for מִן (5:29) keep the existing convention of tagging only the head word (پس / سبب); they could alternatively be grouped as one entry.
- 5:18, 5:21, 5:25, 5:28: "بود" in "... ساله بود که" tagged H2421 (וַיְחִי "lived"), since the Persian renders "lived N years and begot" as "was N years old when he begot"; "که" left untagged (it loosely renders the vav of וַיּוֹלֶד). Doubtful but better than the previous wrong H3205.
- 5:17, 5:20, 5:23, 5:27, 5:31, 5:32: "بود" tagged H1961 (וַיִּהְיוּ / וַיְהִי).
- 5:24 p7-p9: "یافت نشد" grouped for וְאֵינֶנּוּ (H369); "دیگر" left untagged as an added word.
- 5:29 p13: "را" untagged — no אֵת there (object is the suffix of יְנַחֲמֵנוּ). p26-p27: "آسودگی خواهد بخشید" grouped as the rendering of יְנַחֲמֵנוּ (H5162), previously untagged. p6 "و" before "گفت" left untagged (added; לֵאמֹר's Hl is not rendered).
- 5:32 p3: "ساله" kept as H1121 H8141 (בֶּן ... שָׁנָה "son of N years" idiom).
