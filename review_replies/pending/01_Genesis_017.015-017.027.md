- Genesis 17:15 p14/p19: the negation לֹא is carried by the prohibitive prefix of مخوان, so H3808 moved from دیگر ("any more", added) to مخوان with H7121.
- Genesis 17:16 p21–p26: "و از او قومها به وجود خواهم آورد" paraphrases וְהָיְתָה לְגוֹיִם ("she shall become nations"); از left untagged (was Hl), "به وجود خواهم آورد" grouped as H1961. The second را (p18) is untagged because the Hebrew has only a suffix, no אֵת. Not fully sure of this.
- Genesis 17:17 p14: the interrogative ה of הַ/לְּ/בֶן is listed as Hd (morph HTi), so the first آیا is tagged Hd rather than Hi (Hi belongs to o14). p18 را tagged Hl (the "to" of לְבֶן), and پسری counted as added, with مرد → H1121. Not fully sure of this.
- Genesis 17:19 p8: بلکه also tagged H61 (نه، بلکه renders אֲבָל). p15 که tagged Hc (the ו of וְקָרָאתָ). Not fully sure of this.
- Genesis 17:20 p2–p3: "در خصوص" grouped as Hl. The final از وی was left untagged.
- Genesis 17:21 p15: در tagged Hl (it governs همین وقت = לַמּוֹעֵד הַזֶּה). Hb of בַּשָּׁנָה is not rendered.
- Genesis 17:22: ابراهیم sits where אִתּוֹ is, so با → H854. نزد → H5921 (מֵעַל), not H854.
- Genesis 17:23 / 17:26: همان → H6106 kept. It arguably also renders H2088, left as is.
