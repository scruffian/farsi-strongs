- Psalms 42:5 p29 (خدای → H430): taken from the neighbouring verse Psalms 42:6 (o1 אֱלֹהַ/י); the Persian follows the 42:11 wording ("my God") at the end of 42:5. Correspondingly, 42:6's Persian omits "O my God".
- Psalms 42:5 / 42:11: split "اندرونم پریشانی" into "اندرونم" (untagged; with در rendering עָלָי, tagged H5921 on در) and "پریشانی" (H1993). Second چرا in 42:5 kept as H4100 (implied repetition of מַה).
- Psalms 41:3: second half ("all his bed you turn in his sickness") rendered freely as "heal completely"; شفای / خواهد داد tagged H2015, H4904 (bed) left unrendered. Unsure.
- Psalms 41:8: هرگز tagged H3808 H3254 (לֹא יוֹסִיף "will not again"); از untagged (Hb belongs to בּוֹ).
- Psalms 41:12: تو untagged (it is the subject of תָּמַכְתָּ; וַאֲנִי not rendered); جاودانه H5769 only, برقרار خواهی داشت grouped as H5324.
- Psalms 42:1: سراپا untagged (was Korah H7141; superscription not translated); توست tagged H413 (אֵלֶיךָ); ای untagged.
- Psalms 42:4: ایامی loosely renders אֵלֶּה but left untagged (unsure); که tagged H3588; به tagged H5704 (עַד); و before آنان untagged (no Hc in Hebrew).
- Psalms 42:9: Hb moved from به to از (בְּלַחַץ "because of oppression"); بنشینم retagged H3212 (אֵלֵךְ), روز سیاه as rendering of קֹדֵר (H6937 on سیاه).
- Psalms 42:10: خُرد کرده grouped as H7524 (רֶצַח), خصم H6887, چراکه Hb (בְּאָמְרָם).
