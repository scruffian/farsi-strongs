- II Chronicles 20:3 p7–p8: grouped "عزم نمود" as the idiom "set his face" (H5414 H6440).
- II Chronicles 20:7 p15, p26: the negation of הֲלֹא (H3808) carried by both negative verbs; ای (p1) untagged as vocative. Kept را p10 as H853; untagged را p18 (no אֵת).
- II Chronicles 20:10: "از این رو" taken as rendering כִּי (H3588) — uncertain; مصر tagged H776 H4714 for "land of Egypt".
- II Chronicles 20:13 p3 (با): left untagged; may loosely render גַּם (H1571).
- II Chronicles 20:15/17: "ترسان و هراسان مباشید" — the negation H408 put on مباشید only; it covers both prohibitions.
- II Chronicles 20:17 p10–p17: "موضع خود را نگاه داشته" taken as הִתְיַצְּבוּ (H3320), "استوار بایستید" as עִמְדוּ (H5975).
- II Chronicles 20:18 p8 (نهاد): kept H6915, though it may be better untagged as part of the paraphrase "روی بر زمین نهاد".
