Notes:
- Exodus 38:18 p13: split "ریزبافتِ تابیده بود" into ریزبافتِ (H8336, fine linen), تابیده (H7806), بود (untagged). p15 ماهرانه → H4639 kept, loose rendering of מַעֲשֵׂה. p28 همچون given Hl H5980 for לְעֻמַּת. Untagged p5 نخهای, which is added in translation (same in 38:23 p14).
- Exodus 38:21: "ثبت کردند" grouped and tagged H6485 (פֻּקַּד). The Persian does not render עֲבֹדַת (H5656) clearly, so کار (part of "به کار رفته", "used") is now untagged. به (p18) tagged H5921 for עַל פִּי, and زیر tagged Hb for בְּיַד. Not fully sure.
- Exodus 38:22 p16: به before موسی is now untagged. It could be read as rendering אֶת (H853).
- Exodus 38:23: וְאִתּוֹ ("and with him") is rendered loosely by "بِصَلئیل را ... یاری می‌داد". I left it untagged. "یاری می‌داد" could arguably take H854.
- Exodus 38:24: بود (p29) tagged H1961 for וַיְהִי. هدایا tagged H8573 (תְּנוּפָה). "به کار رفت" grouped and tagged H6213 (הֶעָשׂוּי).
- Exodus 38:26: "به سوی" grouped and tagged H5921 (עַל). The phrase "این مقدار نقره از جمعیتی ... گرفته شد" is a loose rendering of לְכֹל הָעֹבֵר, and I left it untagged. به in "به بالا" is untagged.
- Exodus 38:28 p11: برای → Hl kept, although the Hebrew Hl belongs to לָעַמּוּדִים ("for the pillars") rather than "for making". This is doubtful. آنها (אֹתָם) is left untagged as a pronoun.
