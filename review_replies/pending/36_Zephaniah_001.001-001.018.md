- Zephaniah 1:4: p6 "بر" was tagged H5186 although it belongs with "ضد" (עַל). I grouped "بر ضد" as H5921 (twice). I tagged "و نیز" (p31–32) as H5973 (עִם "with"); this rendering is loose. I left "همۀ" (p17) untagged because the Hebrew has no כֹּל there.
- Zephaniah 1:3: I grouped "سنگهای لغزش" as H4384 (it was wrongly H1709 + H4384). I untagged را p4, p12 and p17 because there is no object marker אֵת (H853) before them; the אֶת before הָרְשָׁעִים is H854 "with".
- Zephaniah 1:9: I removed H3605 from p13 "آنان" (there is no כֹּל there) and tagged p17 "خداوند" as H113 (אֲדֹנֵיהֶם "their master"). "از روی" (p7–8) is left as it was: only روی is tagged H5921.
- Zephaniah 1:10: I tagged "صدای" (p20) as H6963, the single קוֹל, which also governs the later nouns; it was H6963 H1419. "شنیده خواهد شد" stays H6963 as it was, but this is uncertain.
- Zephaniah 1:12: I tagged "چُرت می‌زنند" as H7087, the same number as "آسوده‌خیال", because both render קֹפְאִים (doubtful). I removed H853 from p13 and p24 "را" because there is no אֵת there.
- Zephaniah 1:9, 1:10, 1:12: I tagged "آن" as H1931 (הַהוּא/הַהִיא).
- Zephaniah 1:13: I tagged "خواهد رفت" and "خواهد شد" as H1961 (וְהָיָה … לְ), which is somewhat doubtful. I grouped the negated verbs with their verb root, so "ساکن نخواهند شد" is H3808 H3427 and "نخواهند نوشید" is H3808 H8354. I removed H8354 from "شراب".
- Zephaniah 1:14: I swapped the tags of "خیلی" and "زود": خیلی is now H3966 (מְאֹד) and زود is now H4118 (מַהֵר).
- Zephaniah 1:17: "تنگ" was wrongly tagged H5787. I grouped "به تنگ خواهم آورد" as H6887, tagged "کورانه" as Hk H5787, and untagged "زمین" because the Persian adds it.
- Zephaniah 1:18: I left "نه" (p6 and p13) as H1571 H3808, the combined גַּם … לֹא.
- I took no numbers from neighbouring verses.
