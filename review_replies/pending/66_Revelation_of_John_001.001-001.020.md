- Revelation of John 1:16 p24/p25 (درخشش / کاملش): left as G5316 G1411 / G846; the Persian "in its full brilliance" merges φαίνει ἐν τῇ δυνάμει αὐτοῦ, so the split is uncertain.
- Revelation of John 1:17 p12: split the wrong group "او دست" into او (added subject, untagged) and regrouped "دست راستش" → G1188 G846.
