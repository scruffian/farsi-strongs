- Psalms 136:1–13: refrain fixed in every verse: جاودانه was "H5769 H2617" (H2617 belongs to محبت) and is now "Hl H5769" for לְעוֹלָם; the copula است had H5769 and is now untagged.
- Psalms 136:1–7, 10, 13: را after the person thanked (خداوند را / خدای خدایان را / ربّ‌الارباب را / او را که) is tagged Hl, since it renders the ל of הודו ל / לְעֹשֵׂה etc. I'm not sure about this; it could be left untagged instead.
- Psalms 136:1 p8: و tagged H3588, because it renders the second כִּי.
- Psalms 136:3: سپاس + گویید (p3–p4) joined as "سپاس گویید" H3034; گویید had been tagged H113 by mistake.
- Psalms 136:13 p8–p9: "دو" H1506 / "نیم کرد" H1504 kept as they were, though "دو نیم" (H1506) + "کرد" (H1504) would also be defensible.
