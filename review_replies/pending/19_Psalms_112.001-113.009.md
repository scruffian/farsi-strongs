- Psalms 112:5: p9 در had H3557 (יְכַלְכֵּל "conducts/maintains"); the Persian paraphrases ("که در کارهای خویش با انصاف است") with no clear rendering of the verb, so H3557 was removed and left untagged.
- Psalms 112:6: هرگز tagged "Hl H5769 H3808" for the idiom לְעוֹלָם לֹא; the negation is also in "جنبش نخواهد خورد" (H3808 H4131). تا of "تا به ابد" untagged (renders bare עוֹלָם, o6); Hl of לְזֵכֶר moved to در.
- Psalms 112:8: p11 پیروزمندانه untagged as a translator's addition (implied by רָאָה בְּ "look in triumph on").
- Psalms 112:4: second برای (p9) untagged; no ל before חַנּוּן.
- Psalms 113:5/113:6: که / آן که could render the relative article Hd on the participles; left untagged.
