- Deuteronomy 31:3 p18 (تا): tagged Hc for the vav-consecutive of וִירִשְׁתָּם; p21 را untagged (no אֵת). Grouped "به تصرف آورید" and "هلاک خواهد کرد" as compound verbs.
- Deuteronomy 31:4 p2 (با): tagged Hl for לָהֶם; "این قومها" left untagged as rendering the pronoun suffix.
- Deuteronomy 31:5 p6 (دست): idiomatic rendering of לִפְנֵיכֶם, tagged H6440; p19 به (before شما, rendering אֶתְכֶם) left untagged.

- Deuteronomy 31:7 p4 (را): untagged although it renders the ל of לִיהוֹשֻׁעַ; p33 (که) tagged Hl for the infinitive לָתֵת; ملکیت / در خواهی آورد both tagged H5157 (split compound), ایشان (אוֹתָם) left untagged.
- Deuteronomy 31:8: second הוּא (o5) has no clear Persian equivalent; او in "او تو را" left untagged.
- Deuteronomy 31:11 p17 (حاضر می‌شوند): tagged H935 H7200 since it covers both "come" and "appear"; "زمانی که" grouped for בְּ (Hb).
