Notes (unsure cases; no numbers were taken from neighbouring verses):

- Proverbs 30:17 p22 کرکسها: tagged H1121 H5404 because the Persian word renders בְנֵי נָשֶׁר ("sons of the eagle", i.e. vultures). Leaving out H1121 would also be defensible.
- Proverbs 30:18 p4 مرا: tagged H4480 because it renders מִמֶּנִּי ("[too wonderful] for me"). This is a fairly free rendering.
- Proverbs 30:21 p14 تاب / p16 ندارد: the idiom تاب ... ندارد renders לֹא תוּכַל, and تحملشان renders שְׂאֵת. Because تاب and ندارد are not adjacent, I gave both H3201 (rule 5).
- Proverbs 30:22/23 که: tagged H3588 where כִּי ("when") is rendered as a relative که.
- Proverbs 30:23 p1–p4: grouped زن آکنده از نفرت as one rendering of שְׂנוּאָה (H8130). Removed the wrong H8198 from نفرت.
- Proverbs 30:27 p2 را: this را renders the ל of לָאַרְבֶּה, not את. Under rule 7 I left it untagged, though Hl could be argued.
- Proverbs 30:29: tagged وقار/متانت as H3190 (מֵיטִיבֵי, "stately") and راه می‌رود as H6806 (צָעַד). با was left untagged. The mapping is free; grouping با وقار / با متانت as H3190 would be an alternative.
- Proverbs 30:31 p1/p2: خروسِ خرامان renders זַרְזִיר מָתְנַיִם. I split it as خروسِ H2223 and خرامان H4975, which is uncertain. I also split که off سپاهیانش (H510).
- Proverbs 30:33: به دست می‌آید renders the first יוֹצִיא (H3318). The second and third יוֹצִיא are elided in the Persian. Removed the wrong H3588 from the second از and split زیرا from از.
