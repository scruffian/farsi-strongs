- Haggai 1:2 p10 هنوز kept as H3808; the negation is really in p15 فرا نرسیده (tagged H935). Could arguably carry H3808 instead.
- Haggai 1:5, 1:7: grouped نیک بیندیشید → H7760 H3824 (idiom "set your heart"); نیک alone has no separate original word.
- Haggai 1:6 p11/p18 هرگز kept as H369, though the negation is expressed in نمی‌...; for p24 گرم نمی‌شوید added H369 since no هرگز there.
- Haggai 1:8 p14 تا tagged Hc (purpose waw of וְאֶרְצֶה), p15 از tagged Hb (בּוֹ) – somewhat interpretive.
- Haggai 1:9: free paraphrase; "و بر باد شد" treated as added (untagged); حاصل آمد untagged; در حالی که left untagged (circumstantial waw of וְאַתֶּם).
- Haggai 1:12 زیرا که grouped as Hk H834 (כַּאֲשֶׁר), though the Persian reads causal.
- Haggai 1:13 بیان کرده tagged H559 (וַיֹּאמֶר) in addition to گفت H559 (לֵאמֹר); هستם untagged (verbless clause).
- No numbers taken from neighbouring verses.
