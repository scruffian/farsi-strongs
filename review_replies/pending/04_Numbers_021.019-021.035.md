Notes (unsure cases):

- Numbers 21:20 p14 "مشرف" kept as H8259 H6440 (מַשְׁקִיף עַל פְּנֵי rendered as مشرف بر); p10 "نزدیک" left untagged.
- Numbers 21:22 p7 "راه": untagged, because "راه خود را کج نکردن" is the idiom for נָטָה ("turn aside"). H1870 belongs to the later בְּדֶרֶךְ, which is rendered by شاهراه (H1870 H4428, "king's road"). Also p31 "از" was left as H5674, as in 21:23. Arguably it should be untagged.
- Numbers 21:24 p19 "سرحد": untagged. The first "as far as the Ammonites" (עַד בְּנֵי עַמּוֹן) has no גְּבוּל, so the H1366 there belongs to the second سرحد.
- Numbers 21:25 p10 "آنها" left as H428. The Persian compresses "all these cities … in all the cities of the Amorites", so the alignment is loose.
- Numbers 21:26 p10 "قبل": untagged, because it is added in translation ("قبل از آن"). H7223 stays on پیشینِ.
- Numbers 21:28 p20 "اصلی" now H1167 (בַּעֲלֵי בָּמוֹת is rendered "بلندیهای اصلی"). I am fairly sure but not certain.
- Numbers 21:30 p16 "و" left as Hc. There is no ו before אֲשֶׁר עַד מֵידְבָא, so this و together with ویرانی … رسید paraphrases אֲשֶׁר. "ویرانی" also keeps H8074 under rule 5.
- Numbers 21:35 "کسی" was changed to H8300 (שָׂרִיד) and "باقی نماند" grouped as H1115 H7604. It is a judgement call whether the negation goes on کسی or on نماند.
