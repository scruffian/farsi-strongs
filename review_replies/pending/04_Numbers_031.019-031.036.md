- Numbers 31:19 p10–p11 (از شما): no "from" in the Hebrew, so از is untagged; شما tagged H859 from o1 (וְאַתֶּם), which may be loose. p23 خود → H859 (o18 אַתֶּם).
- Numbers 31:19 p2 (از → Hm Hl): kept as it was, for מִחוּץ לַ "outside of".
- Numbers 31:23 "با وجود این" grouped as H389 (אַךְ "nevertheless"). تابِ/تاب → H935 is an idiomatic rendering of "comes into the fire", so it is kept.
- Numbers 31:27: "به دو نیم" and "تقسیم کنید" both tagged H2673 (non-adjacent rendering of וְחָצִיתָ). "شرکت کرده" grouped and tagged H8610, alongside کسانی.
- Numbers 31:30 p10 بگیر "H3947 H270" kept, since אָחֻז "taken" is folded into the verb.
- Numbers 31:32 / 31:36: "عبارت بود از" may render וַיְהִי/וַתְּהִי (H1961). Left untagged (31:36 عبارت → H4557 kept). - Numbers 31:35 p5 یعنی → H4480 kept (partitive מִן). p15 نشده بودند untagged (it was wrongly H2145). It could arguably carry H3808.
