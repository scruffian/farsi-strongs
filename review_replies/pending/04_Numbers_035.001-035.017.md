Notes (doubtful cases):
- Numbers 35:1 p3 and 35:10 p3 (را): tagged H413, since را here renders אֶל with خطاب کرده (there is no אֵת). In 35:10 the old H853 tag was wrong. Leaving them untagged would also be defensible.
- Numbers 35:2 p24 (در → Hl): kept, but doubtful. در اطراف renders סְבִיבֹתֵיהֶם, and the ל of לֶעָרִים is not clearly rendered.
- Numbers 35:2 p15–16: grouped "به جهت" → Hl.
- Numbers 35:5: grouped each "دو هزار" (dual אַלְפַּיִם) → H505. The four در before ضلع are now untagged: the Hb belongs to בָּאַמָּה, which در does not render. Grouped "تعلق خواهد داشت" → H1961 and tagged بدیشان as Hl (לָהֶם). عنوان is now untagged. p2 از "Hm Hl" is kept.
- Numbers 35:6: grouped "افزون بر" → H5921 (the Hc of וַעֲלֵיהֶם is not separately tagged). بگذارید → H5414 (נתן "allow"). In "آن که کسی را کشته است", only کسی and کشته است keep H7523; که and را are untagged.
- Numbers 35:8: grouped "به نسبت" → "Hk H6310". بگیرید is untagged (زیادتر carries H7235). The second شهرهای (p23/p32) keeps H5892 although "cities" is only implied in that clause.
- Numbers 35:12: تا → Hc (וְלֹא). Grouped "پیش از" → H5704. עָמְדוֹ (H5975) has no clear Persian word, so the old H5975 tag on پیش was dropped. این keeps Hd (doubtful).
- Numbers 35:16–17: او → H1931 and است untagged. The second و is untagged because there is no ו before מוֹת. In 35:16, که (p12) could be the Hc of וַיָּמֹת; I left it untagged. 35:17: کشت → H4191 (יָמוּת בָּהּ) and بدان → Hb.
