- I Chronicles 9:4 p9 (بانی): the ketiv בני/מן is listed as H1144. The qere Bani (H1137) is not in the verse's list, so the tag is left as it is.
- I Chronicles 9:9 p13–14: "ראשי אבות לבית אבותיהם" is condensed into سران خاندانهایشان. The tags are split as H7218 H1 / H1004 H1, which is uncertain.
- I Chronicles 9:18 p4 (امروز): tagged H2008 for עַד הֵנָּה ("until now"). p5–6 (به عنوان → Hl) was kept, though it is doubtful.
- I Chronicles 9:19 p25 (چنانکه): tagged Hc for וַאֲבֹתֵיהֶם, which is doubtful.
- I Chronicles 9:22 p6 (محفاظت): given H7778 (לְשֹׁעֲרִים), moved from دروازه‌ها, which is now grouped with آستانۀ as H5592. p3 (اینها): Hd removed.
