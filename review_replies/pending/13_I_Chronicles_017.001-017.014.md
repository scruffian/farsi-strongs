- I Chronicles 17:6 p25 اسرائیل: not in the Hebrew (only עַמִּי), so untagged. p20 را untagged (H853 belongs to עַמִּי, not ایشان).
- I Chronicles 17:8 p23 بزرگ: kept H1419, although the Persian restructures the phrase ("make your name great"); uncertain.
- I Chronicles 17:9 p20–p23 grouped "جا به جا نشوند" (H3808 H7264) and p29–p30 "ستم نخواهند کرد" (H3808 H1086); p26 دیگر narrowed to H3254.
- I Chronicles 17:10 p21–p23 "افزون بر این" left untagged (loosely renders the ו of וָאַגִּד); p28 که untagged.
- I Chronicles 17:13 p3 را tagged Hl (dative "to him", like مرا → Hl). p27 از untagged.
