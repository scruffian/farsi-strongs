- Proverbs 16:17 p5–p7 (به دور می‌ماند): grouped as the rendering of סוּר. The two مراقب entries were swapped to fit the Persian clause order (p9 = נֹצֵר H5341 "guards his way"; p14 = שֹׁמֵר H8104 "keeps his soul").
- Proverbs 16:20 p7–p8 (کامیاب خواهد_شد): grouped as a free rendering of יִמְצָא טוֹב "finds good" (H4672 H2896); unsure whether to split it instead.
- Proverbs 16:25 p17 (می‌انجامد): untagged. "ends in death" is a paraphrase and דַּרְכֵי (H1870) has no clear equivalent. p9 (نظرش) untagged as an addition in translation.
- Proverbs 16:26 p9–p10 (او را): the preposition עָלָיו (H5921) has no clear equivalent, so left untagged. כִּי (H3588) is not translated.
- Proverbs 16:28 p3 (تخم): left untagged as part of the "sow the seed of" idiom for יְשַׁלַּח; it could arguably share H7971.
- Proverbs 16:30 p8–p10 (در سر دارد): left untagged; arguably part of the rendering of לַחְשֹׁב H2803.
- Proverbs 16:32 p19 (برتر): tagged H2896 because it repeats the elided טוֹב of the first clause; unsure.
- Proverbs 16:33 p2–p3 (بر زمین): kept as Hb H2436, although the Persian says "on the ground" and the Hebrew says "into the lap".
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
