Notes:
- Numbers 26:1: the Hebrew data for this verse holds only וַיְהִי אַחֲרֵי הַמַּגֵּפָה. The rest of the Persian (خداوند به موسی و به اِلعازار... گفت) has no numbers in this verse or its neighbours, so it stays untagged. "پس از" is grouped as H310. Hc (of וַיְהִי, which is not translated) is dropped. This is uncertain. The و before اِلعازار is untagged too, since it belongs to the missing words.
- Numbers 26:2 p12 (با → Hb): "with the army of Israel" is taken as rendering בְּ/יִשְׂרָאֵל. Both سپاه and جنگ keep H6635. "سرشماری کنید" is grouped as H5375 H7218 ("lift the head"). The H1 tag was removed from it.
- Numbers 26:4: "قوم را ... سرشماری کنید" is implied and has no Hebrew behind it, so را is untagged (it is not אֵת). I did not take numbers for سرشماری from 26:2.
- Numbers 26:7, 14, 18, 22: every number word (thousands and hundreds included) is put on the digit string in Hebrew order. The Hc "and" inside the numbers is left out. تن is untagged because it has no Hebrew word behind it. This convention is uncertain.
- Numbers 26:9: آنگاه now gets Hb (בְּ+infinitive "when"). که and به are untagged. The second که had H834, but there is no אֲשֶׁר there. The first که (p15) keeps H834 (אֲשֶׁר הִצּוּ), which is uncertain. "بر ضد" is grouped as H5921.
- Numbers 26:10: "در کام کشید" is grouped as H398, and Hb (בַּ/אֲכֹל) is left untranslated. This is uncertain. دویست/پنجاه were corrected to H3967/H2572. "برای دیگران" is an addition and is untagged. Hl of לְנֵס is absorbed in عبرتی. In "همراه با" the tags are left as they were (همراه Hc). This is uncertain.
- "بر حسب" is grouped as one Hl entry throughout (26:2, 12, 15, 18, 20, 22).
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
