Notes:
- Genesis 37:2 p40 (را → H853): את in the Hebrew marks the report (דִּבָּתָם), while the Persian has را on پدر after rewording. I kept H853 but I'm not sure about it. p16: H1961 added because the periphrastic היה + participle is rendered by the continuous شبانی می‌کرد. p34 او stands for Joseph (o25), but I left it untagged. p10 داشت renders the idiom בֶּן … שָׁנָה only loosely, so I left it untagged.
- Genesis 37:3 p14–15 (او بود): הוּא (H1931) is implied in بود; I left it untagged.
- Genesis 37:7 p27 آن tagged H485: it is the only Persian word standing for the noun in לַאֲלֻמָּתִי. p19 بر is part of the "gathered around" phrase, so I left it untagged.
- Genesis 37:8 p6 براستی / p13 حقیقتاً render the infinitives absolute מָלֹךְ (H4427) and מָשׁוֹל (H4910). The second آیا renders אִם (H518). p27 بیشتر might also take H5750.
- Genesis 37:9 p4 دیگر keeps "H5750 H312" (עוֹד + אַחֵר). The second دیگر (p18) renders only עוֹד (H5750).
- Genesis 37:13 چنانکه می‌دانی is grouped as the free rendering of הֲלוֹא (Hi H3808). تا is tagged Hc for וְאֶשְׁלָחֲךָ, which I'm not sure about.
- Genesis 37:14 p31 چون might also be Hc (וַיָּבֹא); I left it untagged.
- Genesis 37:18 بیاید is tagged H7126 together with نزدیک (non-adjacent parts of יִקְרַב). ایشان (אֲלֵיהֶם) and که (Hl of לַהֲמִיתוֹ) are left untagged.
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
