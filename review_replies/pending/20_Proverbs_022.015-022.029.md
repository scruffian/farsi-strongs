- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
- 22:16 p1 "آن که بر" split into separate words, with بر tagged H6231 as part of "بر … ظلم کند". p6 "مال خویش بیفزاید" split, with only بیفزاید tagged H7235. The second بر and خویش (≈ ל/וֹ "for himself") are left untagged; خویش could arguably take Hl.
- 22:19 "توکل کنی": tagged H4009 only. לִהְיוֹת (Hl H1961) is not really rendered, apart from تا, which is tagged Hl.
- 22:20 "سی مَثَل" grouped as H8032 (the qere/sense "thirty [sayings]"). مَثَل could be seen as added.
- 22:21 حقیقی/مطمئن: I took حقیقی as H571 and مطمئن as H7189 (קשט). The pairing is unsure.
- 22:23 "بر خواهد_خاست" grouped and tagged H7378 together with دفاع (one verb, rendered in two places).
- 22:24 آن → H376 and خشم → H2534 for "man of wrath"; تندخو → H639 (בעל אף, with مرد → H1167). "دمساز مشو" split as H935 + H3808.
- 22:25 "در افکنی" → H3947 (paraphrase of "take a snare"). به is left untagged (Hl of לנפשך may be rendered by را/به).
- 22:26 از → Hb ("among those who…"). می‌شوند → H6148 with ضامن.
- 22:27 unchanged. نیز loosely renders לָמָּה ("why"?) and is left untagged.
- 22:29 خواهد_بود → H3320. The second יִתְיַצֵּב is implicit after نه.
