- Nehemiah 3:2–3:7: "וְעַל יָדוֹ/יָדָם" pattern tagged در → H5921, کنار → H3027, pronoun (ایشان/آنان/آنها) untagged as it renders the suffix.
- Nehemiah 3:3, 3:6: קֵרוּהוּ rendered "تیرهای ... کار گذاشتند"; both parts tagged H7136.
- Nehemiah 3:5 p16 ننهادند: renders לֹא הֵבִיאוּ → "H3808 H935".
- Nehemiah 3:12 p16 بود: copula of the relative clause, not הוּא; untagged. p18–19 "همراه با" grouped as Hc (renders וּ before בְנוֹתָיו).
- Nehemiah 3:13, 3:15: final "مرمت کردند/کرد" kept as H2388 (repeats the verb of the verse; Hebrew clause has no verb).
- Nehemiah 3:15 p23 نهاد tagged H2926 with سقفش (rule 5); p42 کنار tagged Hl (לְגַן "by the garden") – somewhat doubtful.
- Nehemiah 3:16 p11 از (in "نیمی از") wrongly had H310; untagged. p21 مصنوعی → H6213 (הָעֲשׂוּיָה).
- Many را entries untagged where no אֵת stands before the object (3:1, 3:3, 3:6, 3:13–3:15).
