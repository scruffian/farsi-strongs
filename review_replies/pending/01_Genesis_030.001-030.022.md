Notes (unsure cases; no numbers were taken from neighbouring verses):

- Genesis 30:1 p8–p9: grouped "فرزندی نیاورد" as H3808 H3205 ("she bore not"); moved H3588 from چون to که, since که renders כִּי. p26 وگرنه: added Hc for וְ/אִם.
- Genesis 30:2 p15 هستم: there is no copula in the Hebrew, so I moved H595 to من (p11).
- Genesis 30:3 p2 راحیل: there is no name in the Hebrew (it is the subject of the verb), so I removed H1090. p14 تا tagged Hc (the וְ of וְ/תֵלֵד, used for purpose). The group "صاحب فرزندان گردم" renders וְ/אִבָּנֶה (H1129, "be built up").
- Genesis 30:6, 30:8, 30:18: نامید → H7121 H8034, matching 30:11/13/20/21. In 30:6 and 30:20 I untagged پسر in "آن پسر را … نامید": no בֵּן stands behind it there.
- Genesis 30:8 p8 سخت → H430 (kept; "wrestlings of God" rendered as an intensifier) and p12 و untagged (might render גַּם). Both are unsure.
- Genesis 30:15 p4 راحیل: it renders the suffix of לָ/הּ, so I untagged it and tagged به → Hl. p8 آیا may render the interrogative ה (listed as Hd); left untagged. p18 اکنون → Hc (the וְ of וְ/לָ/קַחַת) is unsure; p19 می‌خواهی (for the ל + infinitive) left untagged.
- Genesis 30:16 p2 هنگامی: I moved Hb to شامگاهان (בָּ/עֶרֶב). I also grouped "اجیר کرده‌ام" as H7936 (infinitive absolute + finite verb).
- Genesis 30:17 p3 را and Genesis 30:22 p9 را: these mark the object where the Hebrew has אֶל (H413), not אֵת, so they are untagged (H853 removed in 30:22).
- Genesis 30:20 p9–p10 "به من" renders אֹתִ/י (H853); left untagged under rule 7 (unsure). I split p17 so that مرا is untagged and "حرمت خواهد نهاد" → H2082.
