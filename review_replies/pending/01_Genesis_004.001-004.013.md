- Genesis 4:4 p11: "هدیه‌ای" untagged; MT has no מִנְחָה in the bringing clause (it is supplied), the only H4503 is מִנְחָתוֹ rendered by "هدیۀ" (p17). Unsure whether the project prefers to tag such implied repetitions.
- Genesis 4:5: "منظور نداشت" grouped as one compound verb (H3808 H8159); "گشت" tagged H5307 for consistency with 4:6 ("دلریش گشته‌ای"). Unsure whether "دلریش گشت" should instead be one group "H5307 H6440". לְקַיִן Hl left untagged (idiomatic).
- Genesis 4:6 p13: "چرا" renders וְלָמָּה, so given "Hl H4100" (Hc on "و"). "سبب" left untagged.
- Genesis 4:7: "پذیرفته نمی‌شوی" given H3808 H7613 (negation from הֲלוֹא); "انجام دهی"/"انجام ندهی" grouped as part of תֵּיטִיב; "بر در" = לַפֶּתַח (Hl, H6607, previously untagged); "به کمین نشسته" grouped for רֹבֵץ (the "به" was wrongly H413); "توست" tagged H413 for וְאֵלֶיךָ (unsure: could be left untagged as pronoun rendering); "بر" before "آن" corrected from H4910 to Hb (בּוֹ), "چیره شوی" grouped H4910.
- Genesis 4:8 p9–p13: "بیا تا به صحرا برویم" follows the Samaritan/LXX reading not present in MT; untagged "به" and "صحرا" there. "چون" tagged Hb (בִּהְיוֹתָם), "بودند" H1961.
