Notes (unsure cases / neighbouring numbers):
- II John 1:1 p15-16 (دوست می‌دارم): grouped and tagged G1473 G25, treating the explicit ἐγώ as rendered by the verb ending; G25 alone would also be defensible. p24-25 (همۀ آنان که): left as G3956 with که untagged; آنان/که arguably render the article οἱ (G3588), and καί "also" is not rendered.
- II John 1:5 p24 (داشته‌ایم): tagged G1502 as listed, but the source lemma looks wrong: εἴχαμεν is from ἔχω (G2192), not εἴκω. G2192 is not in this verse or its neighbours, so I could not use it.
- II John 1:6 p27 (محبت): tagged G846 because it renders the pronoun αὐτῇ ("in it"), which the Persian takes as love. p22 (او in "حکم او") is untagged because the second clause has no αὐτοῦ. The second ἵνα (o21) is not rendered separately.
- II John 1:7 p9 (که) and p23 (همان): untagged. They loosely render the articles οἱ and ὁ, so G3588 would be possible. The copula ἐστιν is tagged on ضدّمسیح‌اند (G1510 G500).
- II John 1:8 p3-4 (ثمر کار خود): ثمر → G3739 (ἃ "what"), کار → G2038; خود untagged (ἑαυτούς belongs with βλέπετε and is not really rendered here).
- II John 1:9 p1-3 (هر آن کس): left as is (G3956 on هر and on کس); آن could be G3588. p12/p22 (آن) untagged.
- II John 1:12 p11/p13: قلم/کاغذ were swapped. Now قلم (pen) → G3188 μέλαν "ink" and کاغذ → G5489 "paper". p8 لیکن is untagged because ἀλλά is rendered by امّا (p16).
