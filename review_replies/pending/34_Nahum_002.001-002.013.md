- Nahum 2:7: تبعید kept as H1540 (could be H5927 with برده می‌شود); سینه‌زنان given H8608 H3824 (עַל not given); می‌نالند tagged H6963 (כְּקוֹל), ماتم می‌کنند kept H5090.
- Nahum 2:9: و before نه untagged (no ו in that clause); زیرا left untagged although it might render the ו of וְאֵין.
- Nahum 2:11: the second کجاست kept as H346 (repeats the single אַיֵּה).
