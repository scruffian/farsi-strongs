Notes (unsure cases):
- Genesis 39:1 p26–p27: از tagged "Hm H3027" since מִיַּד "from the hand of" is rendered by از alone; H3027 removed from اسماعیلیانی. The partitive از (p16) in "یکی از صاحبمنصبان" is not Hm, so its tag was removed.
- Genesis 39:3 p12: در (Hb) kept, although the Persian restructures "prospered in his hand" (בְּיָדוֹ) as "in whatever he does". Uncertain.
- Genesis 39:4 p4–p7: "شامل حال ... شد" treated as rendering מָצָא (H4672); لطف = חֵן. The second یوسف (p9) is the implied subject of וַיְשָׁרֶת, so it is now untagged. "داشت" given "H3426 Hl" (יֶשׁ לוֹ); چه untagged. Same for 39:6 داشت (Hl, אֲשֶׁר לוֹ) and 39:8 دارد.
- Genesis 39:5 p12/p31: اموال kept as H834 (renders כָּל אֲשֶׁר יֶשׁ לוֹ); could also carry H3426. آن (p18) left untagged, although it may render the article.
- Genesis 39:6 p12: از left untagged. It may render אִתּוֹ (H854).
- Genesis 39:7 p5: "گذشت زمان" for "these things" (H1697) kept loosely. او (p11) now tagged H3130 because it renders יוֹסֵף.
- Genesis 39:9 p18/p22: تو and هستی both keep H859. تو could instead be untagged as the rendering of אוֹתָךְ.
- Genesis 39:10 p2–p3: "با اینکه" may render Hk of כְּדַבְּרָהּ; left untagged.
- Genesis 39:11 p4: چون → Hk (כְּהַיּוֹם) kept, but it may rather render וַיְהִי.
- Genesis 39:13 p16: خانه → H2351 (הַחוּצָה rendered as "from the house") kept.
- Genesis 39:14 p1: خدمتکاران tagged "H376 H1004" (אַנְשֵׁי בֵיתָהּ). Moderately confident.
- Genesis 39:19 p17: چنین tagged "Hk H1697 H428" (כַּדְּבָרִים הָאֵלֶּה). The grouped "سرور یوسف" was split, and یوسف (the rendering of the suffix) is untagged.
- Genesis 39:21 p19: به untagged; Hb of בְּעֵינֵי is effectively rendered by نظر.
- Genesis 39:22: را (p5) untagged because it marks یوسف, not the אֵת object (the prisoners). بر (p6) probably renders בְּיַד but was left untagged. "به دست یوسف انجام می‌گرفت" was retagged as rendering הוּא הָיָה עֹשֶׂה (یوسف H1931, verb "H1961 H6213"; به/دست untagged). This is a restructuring and uncertain.
- Genesis 39:23: "کاری نداشت" grouped as "H369 H7200" (אֵין ... רֹאֶה). را and در in "او را در هر چه" were untagged because there is no אֵת or ב there.
