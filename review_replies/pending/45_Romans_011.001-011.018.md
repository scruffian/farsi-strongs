- Romans 11:2 p29 (نزد) and Romans 11:4 p20-21 (در برابر): render a dative article (G3588) but left untagged, conservatively. p32-33 (و گفت) render TR λέγων, not in the listed words; left untagged.

- Romans 11:13 p1 (روی): G1161 dropped, δέ seen as untranslated; "از آنجا که" grouped G1909 G3745; μὲν οὖν untranslated.
- Romans 11:16: p2 تکه‌ای tagged G536 (with نوبر) as a paraphrase of ἀπαρχή; p35 مقدّسند tagged G40 to match p23 (both are elided in the Greek); p32 پس untagged, with καί moved to نیز.
- Romans 11:17: p14 شاخه‌های tagged G846 (renders αὐτοῖς); "تغذیه می‌کنی" tagged G4791 G1096 as a paraphrase of συγκοινωνὸς ... ἐγένου; "شیرۀ مقوّی" tagged G4096.
