Notes (uncertain cases):
- 32:36 p14–15 grouped "آنگاه که" as the second כִּי (H3588); the third כִּי is not rendered. p24–25 grouped "کסי باقی نمانده" as אֶפֶס (H657).
- 32:38 p21 "را" tagged H5921 because it renders עֲלֵיכֶם ("for you"), not H853. p19 "و" untagged (there is no ו in the Hebrew).
- 32:39 "من خود، اویم": "خود" taken as the second אֲנִי, "اویم" as הוּא (H1931). "جز" = עִמָּדִי (H5978); "دیگر" was added in translation.
- 32:40 p10 "بودنم" tagged H595 (אָנֹכִי, carried by the ending -م). This is uncertain. "سوگند می‌خورم" grouped as אָמַרְתִּי (H559).
- 32:41 p10 "به" untagged: the Persian restructures בְּמִשְׁפָּט, and "به دست" renders יָדִי rather than ב. "خواهم کشید" tagged H7725 (אָשִׁיב).
- 32:43 the Persian follows the longer LXX/Qumran text. The added clauses (heavens, gods, sons of God, "آنان را که از او بیزارند سزا می‌دهد") are left untagged. MT הַרְנִינוּ is moved from "شادی کنید" (p7) to "شادمان باشید" (p26), and יָשִׁיב is moved to "می‌گیرد". "برای" (p60) and the second "و" (p62) are untagged because they were added.
- 32:44 "همراه" tagged Hc for וְהוֹשֵׁעַ ("and Hoshea"). This is uncertain.
- 32:46 "تا" tagged H834 (the second אֲשֶׁר) and "که" tagged Hl (from לִשְׁמֹר). Both are uncertain. "بتوانید" was added and is untagged. "در" before "دل" was added and is untagged. "را" (p7) is untagged because no אֶת precedes it.
- 32:47 "برای شما" = מִכֶּם, so "برای" tagged "Hm H4480". "این" = הוּא (H1931). "شماست" left untagged (the second הוּא is uncertain).
- 32:48 "همان" given "H6106 H2088" (בְּעֶצֶם … הַזֶּה, "that very").
- 32:52 "از این رو" grouped as כִּי (H3588). "پیش روی" = מִנֶּגֶד ("Hm H5048").
- 32:51 "حرمت ننهادید": "حرمت" keeps H6942 and "ننهادید" keeps H3808 as before.
- No numbers taken from neighbouring verses.
