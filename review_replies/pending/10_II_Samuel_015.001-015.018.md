- II Samuel 15:7 p14–15 اجازه دهی: grouped and tagged H4994 (renders the polite request with נָא), unsure; previously H7999/H4994.
- II Samuel 15:8 p17 به‌واقع: tagged H7725 as rendering the infinitive absolute ישיב (emphatic "indeed"); p26–27 در حِبرون has no word in the Hebrew (follows LXX) and is left untagged.
- II Samuel 15:11 p12 میهمان: tagged H7121 together with دعوت شده بودند (קְרֻאִים "invited ones/guests"); unsure, could be left untagged. The previous H8537 moved to ساده‌دلی (לְתֻמָּם).
- II Samuel 15:12: split the wrongly grouped entry "قربانیها تقدیم" into قربانیها (H2077) and grouped "تقدیم می‌کرد" (H2076). را after کسی marks the added "someone", so H853 removed; از (was Hm) untagged and Hm moved to به in "به شهر وی" (renders מֵעִירוֹ, though the Persian says "to" rather than "from") — unsure.
- II Samuel 15:14: grouped "جان به در نخواهیم بُرد" as H3808 H1961 H6413 (לֹא תִהְיֶה לָּנוּ פְלֵיטָה), unsure about the split of H3808 between هیچ‌یک and the verb; را after صاحبمنصبان and after شهر untagged (no אֵת there).
- II Samuel 15:15 p4 (او): tagged H4428 as it stands for the second הַמֶּלֶךְ ("to the king"); could be left untagged if pronoun renderings are not tagged.
