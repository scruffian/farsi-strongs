- Numbers 34:1 p4–p5: grouped "خطاب کرده" as H1696; dropped H413 (no separate Persian word renders אֶל; را is an object marker, not אֵת). Unsure whether H413 should stay on the verb.

- Numbers 34:3 p8–p9: על ידי rendered as در (H5921) + جوار (H3027). شما (לָכֶם) left untagged here and in 34:6 (pronoun suffix); unsure.
- Numbers 34:4 p4–p5: grouped "گَردَنۀ عَقرَبیم" → H4610 (Maaleh-Akrabbim). Added H1961 to خواهد بود and H3318 to رفته.
- Numbers 34:5 p2 / 34:11 p1 / 34:12 p2: این renders the article in places. I left these tags as they were (Hd only in 34:12), so they are inconsistent.
- Numbers 34:6 p10 است: tagged H1961 (יִהְיֶה); p7 خواهد بود H1961.
- Numbers 34:7 p11 / 34:10 p7 تا: removed Hl (there is no ל in the Hebrew; it is only directional he or apposition).
- Numbers 34:8 p13–p14: grouped "ادامه خواهد یافت" → H1961 H8444 (וְהָיוּ תּוֹצְאֹת); unsure. תְּתָאוּ (H8376) is not rendered.
- Numbers 34:11: "رو به پایین" and "کشیده شده" left as before. به موازات (על) left untagged; it might take H5921. امتداد (wrongly H1366) is grouped with خواهد یافت → H4229.
- Numbers 34:12 p21 چنین → H2063 (זֹאת).
- Numbers 34:13: the second سرزمینی (p20) is a translator's repetition but keeps H776. قبیله changed to H4294 only. داده (p24) was H5414 and is now part of "فرمان داده" H6680; داده شود → H5414.
