- II Chronicles 1:5 p13 (آنجا): Hebrew שָׂם "he put" (H7760) is read by the Persian as שָׁם "there"; tagged آنجا with H7760 and removed H7760 from ساخته بود. Unsure.
- II Chronicles 1:1 p21 (به‌غایت): given "Hl H4605" for לְמָעְלָה; could also be H4605 alone.
- II Chronicles 1:11-12 (مال / ثروت): tagged by meaning, ثروت = עֹשֶׁר H6239 "wealth", مال = נְכָסִים H5233 "possessions"; the Persian order is the reverse, so the opposite assignment is possible.
- II Chronicles 1:12 p20-21 (آن سان): grouped and tagged H3651 (כֵן "so"); unsure, it may be better left untagged with H3651 only on مانندِ.
- II Chronicles 1:11 p50-51: split the wrongly grouped "ایشان نصب کرده‌ام" so that the pronoun ایشان (suffix of עָלָיו) stays untagged.
- II Chronicles 1:13: left unchanged. The Hebrew says "to (לַ) the high place" but the Persian has "از مکان بلند" (from); از currently carries Hl, and the به before اورشلیم carries Hl although יְרוּשָׁלִַם has no prefix. Doubtful, but the Persian follows the sense of the verse.
- II Chronicles 1:17 p29 (اینها → H3651): kept, a loose rendering of כֵן; unsure.
- II Chronicles 1:14 p19 (که): tagged Hc for וַ/יַּנִּיחֵ/ם; unsure.
