- John 21:15 p40, 21:16 p3/p32, 21:17 p3/p23/p55, 21:19 p1/p18: "عیسی" is a subject added in translation (Greek has only λέγει/εἶπεν αὐτῷ), so left untagged; αὐτῷ tagged on او where rendered.
- John 21:15 p12, 21:16 p9: vocative "ای" wrongly had G3483 (ναί); untagged.
- John 21:17 p46–p47: split "چیز آگاهی" and regrouped as "همه چیز" G3956 + "آگاهی" G1492.
- John 21:21 p1: kept "چون" G3767 (οὖν), doubtful; it may rather render the participle ἰδών.
- John 21:22/23: αὐτόν (G846) moved from "بخواهم" to "باقی بماند" (as "G846 G3306"), since it is the subject of μένειν.
- John 21:23 p34–p36 "تو را چه": not in the main Greek of 21:23 (it is in 21:22 and in some manuscripts of 21:23); left untagged. "حال آنکه" left untagged (could render καί/δέ).
- John 21:25: χωρῆσαι is missing from the variant word list, so "گنجایش ... می‌داشت" left untagged; removed G975 from "گنجایش" and G2532 from the second "نیز".
- John 21:20 p35: "که" retagged G3588 (ὁ παραδιδούς), following the convention of 21:24 p4.
