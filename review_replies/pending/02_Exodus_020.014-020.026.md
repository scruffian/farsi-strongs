- Exodus 20:15, 20:16, 20:17 (p6, p28), 20:20 (p7, p24), 20:23 (p6, p12), 20:25 (p12), 20:26 (p7): negative imperative light verbs (مکن، مده، مورز، مدار، مترسید، نکنید، مسازید، مساز، مرو) carry the negation number plus the verb, following the existing pattern of 20:14 (مکن → H3808 H5003). Grouping noun+light verb into one entry would be an alternative.
- Exodus 20:17 p25 (او): tagged H7453 as rendering לְרֵעֶךָ ("belonging to your neighbour"); unsure, could be left untagged.
- Exodus 20:18 p13 (شنیدند): no hearing verb in the Hebrew; tagged H7200 as the Persian's rendering of רֹאִים for the sounds. p25 (جملگی) → H3605 for וְכָל הָעָם. p31 (دور) → Hm H7350 since no separate از. p33 (و) ends the Persian verse but begins 20:19 (וַ/יֹּאמְרוּ, Hc) – number taken from the neighbouring verse (same Hc).
- Exodus 20:20 p11/p15 (تا): given all parts of לְבַעֲבוּר / וּבַעֲבוּר; p20 (دل) → H6440 for עַל פְּנֵיכֶם (idiomatic, unsure); p18 را untagged (no אֵת); p22 و untagged.
- Exodus 20:21 p12-13 (در آن): grouped for שָׁם.
- Exodus 20:24 p35 را and 20:25 p17, p24 را untagged: objects are pronoun suffixes or have no אֵת.
- Exodus 20:26 p9 (مبادا) → H834 H3808 for אֲשֶׁר לֹא "lest"; p5 از → Hb (בְּמַעֲלֹת).
