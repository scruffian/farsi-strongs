- Numbers 18:17 p30-31: grouped "به جهت" as one entry for the Hl of לְרֵיחַ.
- Numbers 18:18 p12: "از آنِ تو" taken as rendering לְךָ; tagged از as Hl (the verse has two לְךָ + יִהְיֶה, rendered once).
- Numbers 18:19 p13-14: "کنار گذاشته می‌شود" seems to be an added paraphrase (not נָתַתִּי, which is بخشیده‌ام); untagged. p31 این tagged H1931 (הִוא).
- Numbers 18:20 p18-19: "تو را" renders לְךָ, so را → Hl and تو untagged (was H589); H589 moved to من (p30).
- Numbers 18:22 p1-3: grouped "از این پس" as H5750 (עוֹד); p12 مبادا left untagged (no Hebrew "lest"; it loosely renders the purpose ל of לָשֵׂאת/לָמוּת), unsure. p16 شده tagged H5375 as the second half of "متحمل ... شده".
- Numbers 18:23/18:24: "میراثی نخواهد بود" for לֹא יִנְחֲלוּ נַחֲלָה: noun → H5159, verb → H3808 H5157. This is a judgement call.
- Numbers 18:24 p6-7: "به عنوان" untagged (תְּרוּמָה has no prefix); p20 بنابراین → H5921 H3651 (עַל כֵּן); p22 دربارۀ → Hl (לָהֶם).
- Numbers 18:26 p11-12: "به عنوان" grouped and tagged Hb for בְּנַחֲלַתְכֶם; unsure.
- Numbers 18:28 p24: ده‌یک‌ها is an explanatory addition for the suffix of מִמֶּנּוּ; untagged (previously H8641). Could arguably be H4643.
- Numbers 18:29: the Persian is a loose paraphrase; merged כֹּל ... תְּרוּמַת יְהוָה; "دریافت می‌کنید" untagged as added.
- Numbers 18:30 p1: بنابراین tagged Hc (וְ of וְאָמַרְתָּ); p7 ”آنگاه tagged Hb (infinitive בַּהֲרִימְכֶם); unsure. ממנו (H4480) not tagged (آن in "قسمت آن" read as a suffix).
- Numbers 18:31: H7939 שָׂכָר has no clear Persian word ("آنچه ... دریافت می‌کنید" paraphrases it); left untagged. "که خواستید" is added, untagged.
- Numbers 18:32 p4: هدایا may render מִמֶּנּוּ (H4480); left untagged. p13 نخواهید شد → H3808 H5375 (negated compound verb with متحمل).
