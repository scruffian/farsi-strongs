- All verses follow the same pattern; the same conventions are applied throughout. No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
- "به جهت" (for) is grouped as one entry → Hl in every verse (previously only جهت was tagged).
- 7:23/29/35/41: "گاو نر", "بز نر", "برۀ نرینۀ" grouped as single animal terms (نر/نرینۀ is part of the rendering, not a separate word); "یک ساله" → H1121 H8141 (בְּנֵי שָׁנָה). Removed spurious H352/H6260 doublings and stray H3532 on یک.
- 7:24/30/36/42: هدیۀ was wrongly tagged H5387; there is no "offering" word in the Hebrew, so it is untagged. "بنی‌X" → H1121 + tribe (לִבְנֵי; the ל is not rendered).
- 7:25/31/37/43: نقره → H3701 only; the first مثقال (p11) and the second وزن (p17) and the و before "یک ظرف" have no Hebrew word behind them and are untagged; "هر دو" grouped → H8147; "هدیۀ آردی" grouped → H4503 (was partly H7133).
- 7:26/32/38/44: وزن and مثقال are added in translation (Hebrew just "ten [shekels]"); untagged (were H4392/H2091).
- 7:27/33/39: "گوسالۀ نر" grouped → H6499 H1121 H1241 (פַּר בֶּן בָּקָר). Unsure whether H1121 should be included; گوساله ("young bull") is taken to render בֶּן בָּקָר. Removed H3532 from قوچ in 7:33.
- 7:28/34/40: "بز نر" grouped → H8163 H5795 (שְׂעִיר עִזִּים).
