- Proverbs 13:2 p6 (split "نصیب نیکو"): نصیب … می‌برد tagged H398 ("eats") as a periphrasis; نیکو H2896. Reasonable but interpretive.
- Proverbs 13:3 p12 (آن که → H6589 instead of H5341): the second "he who" is the subject of פֹּשֵׂק; می‌گوید left untagged (part of the rendering of "opens wide his lips").
- Proverbs 13:4 p8-p10 grouped "به دست نمی‌آورد" with H369 (the negation renders וָאַיִן); p13 شخص tagged H5315 (וְנֶפֶשׁ "soul of the diligent"). Unsure about both.
- Proverbs 13:5 p3 دروغ given H1697 H8267 (דְּבַר שֶׁקֶר); p11-p13 grouped "به بار می‌آورد" kept H887 only, although it arguably also carries H2659.
- Proverbs 13:6 p8/p10: the Persian renders רִשְׁעָה as the subject (گناه → H7564) and חַטָּאת (sin, for the sinner) as the object (شریران → H2403); p3 راستان given H8537 H1870 ("perfect of way").
- Proverbs 13:7: free rendering; بی‌چیزی → H369 H3605, "تظاهر به دولتمندی می‌کند" → H6238, "تظاهر به فقر" → H7326 (previous tags had these swapped). با وجود / دیگری / با left untagged.
- Proverbs 13:8: free rendering. ثروتمند → H6239 (his wealth), H6239 removed from جان; نخواهد_کرد → H3808; H376 and H8085 left unrendered.
- Proverbs 13:10 p1 از → Hb (בְּזָדוֹן), p6 برنخیزد → H5414 (יִתֵּן).
- Proverbs 13:11: "بادآورده" → Hm H1892 (wealth "from vanity"), آسان untagged; "بر باد می‌رود" → H4591; "اندک اندک" → H5921 H3027 (עַל יָד, "little by little"); مالش untagged (implied subject).
- Proverbs 13:15 p9-p11 "به جایی نمی‌رسد" → H386: the Persian follows a different sense of אֵיתָן (or the LXX/emended reading); doubtful.
- Proverbs 13:17 p12 ارمغان untagged (no verb or noun behind "brings" in Hebrew; H4832 kept on شفا).
- Proverbs 13:18 p13 عزّت → H3513 (was H8104), p14 نصیب untagged; split "توبیخ گوش" into توبیخ H8433 and grouped "گوش بسپارد" H8104.
- Proverbs 13:20 p1 همنشینِ → H1980 H854 ("walks with").
- Proverbs 13:21: "در پی" and خواهد_بود both H7291 (pursues); نیکبختی → H2896; H853 not rendered.
- Proverbs 13:23: free rendering. چه بسا untagged (was H3426/H7230), فراوان → H7230, دهد untagged, بی‌عدالتی → Hb H3808 H4941, "از میان می‌برد" → H5595. کوچکِ / شخص left untagged; Hc on اما from וְיֵשׁ (H3426 itself not rendered).
