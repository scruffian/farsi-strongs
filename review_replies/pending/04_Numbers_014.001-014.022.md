Notes:
- Numbers 14:1 p7 (بلند کردند): given both H5375 and H5414, since the Persian merges "lifted up and gave their voice" into one verb. p10 (آن) tagged H1931 for ההוא.
- Numbers 14:3 p1 (چرا): tagged "Hl H4100" for לָמָה. p20 (یغما برده شوند): added H1961 for יִהְיוּ, which the Persian renders with شوند. Unsure. p12 (ضرب) is left untagged as an addition in "به ضرب شمشیر".
- Numbers 14:7 p6–7 grouped as "خطاب کرده" and tagged H413, the verb that renders "addressed (אֶל)". Unsure whether it should also take H559 (לֵאמֹר). The preceding را is now untagged because there is no אֵת there.
- Numbers 14:9 p13/p16: tagged ایشان H1992 and left the copula هستند untagged. p28 (از) is untagged because Hm belongs to מֵעֲלֵיהֶם, which is not rendered separately.
- Numbers 14:10 p6 (باید): untagged. It is a modal used to render the infinitive לִרְגּוֹם, so it could arguably take Hl.
- Numbers 14:14: p34–35 (نیز شنیده‌اند که) repeat the earlier "heard that" in the Persian. I kept H8085 on شنیده‌اند and gave که H3588, but this is doubtful. "این قوم" (p40–41) now has no tag, because it expands the suffix in עֲלֵיהֶם. The و at p22 is untagged because the Persian adds it. The אַתָּה at o24 has no Persian word for it.
- Numbers 14:16: split "بدیشان قسم خورده بود" into بدیشان (Hl) and قسم خورده بود (H7650). Gave چون Hm and نتوانست "H1115 H3201".
- Numbers 14:18: هرگز is tagged H5352 for the emphatic infinitive absolute, and "بی‌سزا نمی‌گذارد" is tagged "H3808 H5352". تقصیرکار is untagged as an added object. می‌رساند takes H6485 together with جزای (rule 5). تا takes the second עַל, and پشت is untagged.
- Numbers 14:21 p1 (اما): tagged "Hc H199" for וְאוּלָם.
- Numbers 14:22 p26 (مرا): tagged H853 because it contains the را that marks אֹתִי. Unsure whether the fused form should carry the tag.
- Wherever the original has הַ/זֶּה or הַ/זֹּאת, I removed Hd from این, so این carries only the demonstrative's number (rule 6).
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
