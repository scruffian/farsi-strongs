Notes:
- Genesis 27:3 p16 (چیزی): tagged H6720 (צידה, the game/food to hunt), since چیزی stands for the object; somewhat loose.
- Genesis 27:4 p15/p17, 27:10 p8, 27:19 p27: تا treated as rendering בַּעֲבוּר (Hb H5668, plus H834 in 27:10). In 27:4 the Persian order (تا بخورم و جانم …) does not match the Hebrew closely, so the تا/و split between Hc and Hb H5668 is uncertain.
- Genesis 27:4 p19-20, 27:7 p12-13, 27:10 p12-13: grouped پیش از as a compound preposition (בְּטֶרֶם / לִפְנֵי).
- Genesis 27:5 p1 (چون): tagged Hb (בְּדַבֵּר "when … spoke"); the Hc of וְרִבְקָה is not rendered.
- Genesis 27:12 p9 (بنماید) → H1961 with چنین left untagged; p13-14 grouped به ریشخند گرفته‌ام; p18-19 grouped به جای → Hc H3808 ("and not" rendered as "instead of").
- Genesis 27:14 p10 (رِبِکا): tagged H517 because the name renders אִמּוֹ "his mother".
- Genesis 27:20 p23-25 grouped بر سر راهم → Hl H6440 (לְפָנָי), p26-27 قرار داد → H7136; idiomatic rendering.
- Genesis 27:20 p12 (این) left untagged; the H2088 of מַה זֶּה is arguably part of چگونه … به این زودی.
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
