Notes:
- 11:1 p6 and 11:13 p21, 11:16 etc.: the waw-consecutive Hc on verbs without a Persian و is left untranslated (not added to the verb).
- 11:3 p8 در tagged "Hb H8432" (בְּתוֹךְ rendered by در alone); "بر ضد" grouped as Hl.
- 11:4 "آنگاه که" grouped as Hb (בְּרָדְפָם "when they pursued"); را before تعقیب untagged (H310 "after" has no clear Persian equivalent; it is absorbed into تعقیب کردن). چگونه (p30) untagged as there is no אשר there.
- 11:6 p25 با tagged Hc (וְאֶת "and" with houses); H853 not tagged since it is not را. p33 همراه tagged "Hb H7272" (בְּרַגְלֵיהֶם) - somewhat free.
- 11:8 "به تصرف آورید" grouped as one compound verb (H3423); the Hc of וִירִשְׁתֶּם is not rendered. آن in "برای گرفتن آن" is the suffix, untagged.
- 11:10 H7272 (בְרַגְלְךָ "with your foot") is not rendered in Persian; removed from می‌کردید. را after آن (p30) untagged: no אֵת there.
- 11:13 p9 به tagged H853 (renders אֶתְכֶם "you" as indirect object) - uncertain; could be left untagged. براستی tagged H8085 (infinitive absolute שָׁמֹעַ).
- 11:14 p12 باران is an explanatory addition ("namely the early and late rain"), untagged; تا tagged Hc for וְאָסַפְתָּ - uncertain.
- 11:12 left unchanged; آن after از (rendering אֹתָהּ) left untagged.
