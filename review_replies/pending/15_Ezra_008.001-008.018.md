- Ezra 8:3-7, 8:13, 8:14: joined "همراه با" into one entry tagged H5973 (as already done in 8:8-12); the Hc of וְעִמּוֹ is not separately rendered. In 8:3 the second "با" (با او ثبت شدند) also kept H5973.
- Ezra 8:10 p2-p3: "بَنی" is a name added from the LXX (not in MT), so untagged; "خاندان" now carries H1121 (as with زاتّو in 8:5).
- Ezra 8:1 p7: "با" in "کسانی که با ایشان ثبت شدند" renders the genealogy clause, not עִמִּי (which is p16), so untagged; p12 "ایام" had a wrong H3187, untagged.
- Ezra 8:15 p30/p34: negation moved from "کسی" to "نیافتم" (H3808 H4672).
- Ezra 8:17 p29: "به آنها" paraphrases בְּפִיהֶם; tagged "به" Hb only (unsure). p40 "نزد ما" = לָנוּ, retagged Hl.
- Ezra 8:18 p30-31: "همراه با" grouped and tagged Hc (the ו of וּבָנָיו); p39 "نزد" retagged Hl (לָנוּ). "از آنجا که" (p1-3) paraphrases כְּ; left untagged.
