Notes (unsure cases):
- Leviticus 11:25 p1 "هر کس هر": left as one group tagged H3605; the second هر belongs with "قسمت از" (the partitive מִן), so the grouping is loose.
- Leviticus 11:26 p7–p9 "سُمَش شکاف کامل": I tagged شکاف as H8156 (the noun "cleft") and کامل as H8157 (the cognate verb "cleaving"). This is an approximate match. سُمَش is left untagged as implied.
- Leviticus 11:26 p13 "نمی‌کند": tagged H369 H5927 because the negation is carried by the verb.
- Leviticus 11:27/29/31 "از میان": grouped and tagged Hb ("among"). The Hebrew בְּ here has a partitive sense.
- Leviticus 11:30: the animal names are matched in order (anaqah = مارمولک, koach = سوسمار زمینی, letaah = سوسمار معمولی, chomet = بزمجه, tinshemet = آفتاب‌پرست). قبیل is untagged. The identifications are traditional and uncertain, but the order is clear.
- Leviticus 11:32 p33 "از (آن)": tagged Hb for בָּהֶם ("with them"). This is doubtful.
- Leviticus 11:35 p15 "خواه": tagged Hc for וְ/כִירַיִם. p27 "خواهند ماند" is tagged H1961.
- Leviticus 11:36: "با این همه" is grouped as H389. "است" (p15) is now tagged H1961 for יִהְיֶה, and "باشد" inside the relative clause is untagged, because מִקְוֵה is paraphrased.
- Leviticus 11:42 p14 "یا": left untagged. It loosely renders עַד ("up to"), so this is unsure. "دست و پا" is added in translation and is untagged.
- Leviticus 11:43: the first "خود" is tagged H5315. In the second clause, "خویشتن را" renders the Hithpael reflexive (there is no אֵת or נֶפֶשׁ), so it is untagged. The negation in "نجس مشوید" is the translator's choice and is tagged H2930 only.
- Leviticus 11:44: the negation of לֹא is moved onto "نجس مسازید" (H3808 H2930), so "هیچ" is tagged H3605. In 11:44 and 11:45, "هستم" keeps its existing H589 tag.
