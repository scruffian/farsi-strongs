- Proverbs 26:2 p11 نیز: tagged H3651 (כֵּן "so"), as همچنان in 26:1; the second Hk and the infinitive Hl prefixes are not rendered separately. p12–p15 بر کسی قرار نگیرد: grouped قرار نگیرد for לא תָבֹא; بر کسی treated as added.
- Proverbs 26:4 p11 همچون: tagged Hl (לּ/וֹ "to him" = "like him"); somewhat interpretive.
- Proverbs 26:7 مرد لنگ grouped as H6455 (adjective used substantively); Hm and Hc untranslated; از tagged Hb (בְּ/פִי rendered "from the mouth").
- Proverbs 26:9 از tagged Hb on the same reasoning; بوتۀ خار grouped for H2336.
- Proverbs 26:10 کمانداری tagged H7227 (רַב "archer"), بی‌هدف taken as rendering כֹּל ("wounds all"), so the group gets H2342 H3605; uncertain paraphrase. یا tagged Hc (second וְ); the first וְ (آن که) left untagged.
- Proverbs 26:12 مرد left untagged (added before نادان); could be grouped with نادان.
- Proverbs 26:13 در خانه می‌ماند و and در کمین است are additions, left untagged.
