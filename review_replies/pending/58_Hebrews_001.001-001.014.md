Notes:
- Hebrews 1:2: The Persian verse division differs from the Greek. Persian 1:2 begins with the second half of Greek 1:1 (ἐπ’ ἐσχάτου τῶν ἡμερῶν τούτων ἐλάλησεν ἡμῖν ἐν υἱῷ). I took these numbers from Hebrews 1:1: p2 در G1909, p3 این G3778, p4 ایام G2250, p5 آخر G2078, p6–7 به واسطۀ G1722, p8 پسر G5207, p11 ما G2249, p12–13 سخن گفته_است G2980. For the same reason, ما (p14) in Persian 1:1 is now untagged, because "our fathers" has no pronoun in the Greek.
- Hebrews 1:1 p12 / 1:3 p13 / 1:5 p5: I left با/به for the dative untagged (no G3588), following the Philemon worked example.
- Hebrews 1:3 p8 اوست: tagged G1510 G846 (ὢν + αὐτοῦ). It is unsure whether است should carry G1510. p19 او: kept G3739 as a second rendering of ὃς. p29 مقام: treated as added.
- Hebrews 1:4: The Persian reverses the two comparisons. I tagged by word meaning: برتر = διαφορώτερον G1313, والاتر = κρείττων G2909, برخوردار شد = γενόμενος G1096 (a loose rendering), and فرشتگان/آنها by their own words (G32/G846).
- Hebrews 1:5 p25 یا: kept G3825 (πάλιν), but this is a loose rendering.
- Hebrews 1:7 p1–2 حال آنکه: left untagged. It may render καὶ … μέν, which is unsure.
- Hebrews 1:8 p4 می‌گوید: left untagged. The verb is only implied from 1:7. عدل و انصاف is a doublet for εὐθύτητος; و is untagged.
- Hebrews 1:12: The Persian follows the WHNU variant (ἑλίξεις … ὡς ἱμάτιον). The opening καί is not rendered.
- Hebrews 1:13 p22 زیر: left untagged. It could be part of ὑποπόδιον.
- Hebrews 1:14 p10 به: tagged G1223 (διὰ "for the sake of") as a loose rendering, which is unsure. نیستند carries G3780 G1510, and مگر keeps G3780.
