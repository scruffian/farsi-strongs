Notes:
- Exodus 26:19 p2 (برای): no verb in the Persian for תַּעֲשֶׂה; the old H6213 tag was removed and برای left untagged. p14 زبانۀ now gets only H3027.
- Exodus 26:20 p11 (بساز) left untagged: the verse has no verb; it is supplied from the context of 26:18. p6 یعنی untagged (it does not render ל of לִפְאַת).
- Exodus 26:22 p2 قسمت left untagged; "قسمت انتهایی" could arguably be grouped as H3411.
- Exodus 26:24: the Persian restructures the verse. p13 در ("در پایین") might render Hm Hl of מִלְּמַטָּה; left untagged. The first "به هم" (p15-p16) is read as part of rendering תֹאֲמִים, and the second هم (p28) gets H3162 (וְיַחְדָּו). p29 بسته شوند = יִהְיוּ תַמִּים (H1961 H8535) is an interpretive choice. p24 یک left untagged, and واحد carries H259.
- Exodus 26:31: ماهرانه tagged H4639 H2803 (מַעֲשֵׂה חֹשֵׁב), and دوخته شده باشد tagged H6213 (יַעֲשֶׂה). نقش untagged. Uncertain.
- Exodus 26:33: the Hebrew בֵּין ... וּבֵין is rendered by "از ... جدا خواهد کرد". از is now H996 and the grouped verb is H914. p10 پشت = מִבֵּית (Hm H1004).
- Exodus 26:35: the Persian reorders the verse. "قرار بده" covers both וְשַׂמְתָּ and תִּתֵּן (H7760 H5414), and بگذار for the lampstand is tagged H7760 (the lampstand is also an object of וְשַׂמְתָּ). آن (p15) renders הַשֻּׁלְחָן and is tagged H7979. Uncertain.
- Exodus 26:29 p12 نگاه (H1004 for בָּתִּים, "holders") was kept, but it is loose.
- No numbers were taken from neighbouring verses.
