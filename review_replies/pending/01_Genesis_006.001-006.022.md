Notes (unsure cases; no numbers taken from neighbouring verses):
- Genesis 6:1 p8–p9: grouped فزونی گرفت as one compound verb with "H2490 H7231" (began + to multiply); H2490 ("began") is not otherwise rendered. بر (p6) might also carry H6440 (עַל פְּנֵי); left as H5921.
- Genesis 6:2 p15: به untagged — "به زنی گرفتن" is the Persian idiom for taking as wife; לָהֶם (Hl) is not rendered. p12 را untagged: no אֵת in this clause.
- Genesis 6:3 p13: زیرا tagged "Hb Hs H1571" for בְּשַׁגַּם; که left untagged. موجودی (p16) could arguably share H1320 with فانی.
- Genesis 6:4: split the reading of پس از آن as پس از → H310, آن → H3651; آن (p2) → H1992 (הָהֵם). فرزندان kept H3205 though it is a supplied object of the verb.
- Genesis 6:7 p26–p27: که is not אֲשֶׁר; the second כִּי ("that I made them") is rendered by از ساختن, so از → H3588 (uncertain). p23 را kept H853 as a repeat of the same object marker for the appositional list.
- Genesis 6:11 p1: باری possibly renders the narrative וַ (Hc); left untagged. p12 شده بود: H430 was wrong; left untagged (auxiliary of فاسد/آکنده).
- Genesis 6:13: به سبب = מִ/פְּנֵי (به → Hm, سبب → H6440); از in "آکنده از خشونت" has no Hebrew behind it. آنان را is the object suffix of מַשְׁחִיתָם, untagged.
- Genesis 6:14 p12–p13: در kept H854 (the אֶת before הַתֵּבָה); آن → H8392. The و before حجره‌هایی has no Hebrew ו.
- Genesis 6:16 p11: بساز carries H6213; H3615 (תְּכַלֶּנָּה "finish it") is not clearly rendered. p20 و kept Hc though the Hebrew ו belongs to וּפֶתַח.
- Genesis 6:17 p1: زیرا may loosely render וַ/אֲנִי's Hc; left untagged.
- Genesis 6:20 p10: و kept Hc (Hebrew וּ is on וּמִן before the beasts; Persian places و before the creeping things).
- Genesis 6:21 p11: split تا برای; تا → Hc (וְהָיָה), برای → Hl.
