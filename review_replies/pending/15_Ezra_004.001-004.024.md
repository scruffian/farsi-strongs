- Ezra 4:2 p43-p44 (به او): follows the Qere ולו "to him" rather than Ketiv ולא (o15 listed as Hc + H3808); left به untagged. p21 ما (H587) kept, though אֲנַחְנוּ stands in the later clause.
- Ezra 4:3 p14-p16 (شما را با ما): tagged را → Hl and با → Hc Hl for לָכֶם וָלָנוּ; somewhat interpretive.
- Ezra 4:4 p14 از → Hl (infinitive לִבְנוֹת) is interpretive.
- Ezra 4:7 p14 نامه‌ای kept H3791 (current tag; the first clause has only the verb כָּתַב); NMV's single "نامه" later renders הַנִּשְׁתְּוָן; the Persian has one آرامی for both אֲרָמִית.
- Ezra 4:9: realigned the gentilic list by order (Dinaites=قاضیان, Apharsathchites=بازرسان, Tarpelites=صاحبمنصبان, Apharsites=پارس, Erech, Babylon, Susa); the mapping of these obscure terms to NMV's renderings is uncertain.
- Ezra 4:13: جزیه/باج/خراج mapped by order to מִנְדָּה/בְלוֹ/הֲלָךְ; uncertain.
- Ezra 4:14 p22 برای (Hl) kept as it was; loose rendering. כְּעַן not rendered.
- Ezra 4:20: tribute terms realigned by order (جزیه=מִדָּה, باج=בְלוֹ, خراج=הֲלָךְ), as in 4:13; uncertain.
- Ezra 4:22: "در این کار سستی نکنید" mapped as در=עַל, کار=לְמֶעְבַּד, سستی نکنید=שָׁלוּ; free rendering, somewhat uncertain.
- Ezra 4:23 p24 آنان → H1994 (הִמּוֹ, the object pronoun "them").
