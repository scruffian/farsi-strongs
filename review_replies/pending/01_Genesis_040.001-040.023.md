Notes (unsure cases):
- Genesis 40:1 p1–p2 (چندی بعد): "a while after" is a loose rendering of "after these things"; چندی is tagged H1697 H428 and بعد H310. וַיְהִי is not rendered.
- Genesis 40:3 p5–p8: زندانِ is tagged H4929 and دربار is tagged H1004 (the house of the captain of the guard). "زندانی کرد" (put in prison) is grouped as H5414 H5470; בֵּית (o8) and אֶל are not rendered separately. "در بند بود" is grouped as H631.
- Genesis 40:5: شبی is tagged H3915 H259 (one night). "هر دو" (both) is grouped as H8147 and "هر یک" (each) as H376. "در بند بودند" is grouped as H631; the second در is not Hb.
- Genesis 40:7: "از چه سبب" is grouped as H4069. הַיּוֹם (today) is not rendered, so the H3117 tag on دارید is removed. לֵאמֹר is not rendered.
- Genesis 40:8: "از آنِ" is grouped as Hl ("belongs to God"). There is no Hebrew word for "dream" or את in the last clause, so خوابتان and را are untagged.
- Genesis 40:10: Persian reorders the clauses. The و at p9 has no Hebrew ו, so it is untagged. که (≈ וְהִיא) is left untagged. Both رسیده and "به بار آورد" carry H1310.
- Genesis 40:13: پیشینت keeps H7223, which the translator seems to have moved from כַּמִּשְׁפָּט הָרִאשׁוֹן. زمانی (≈ H4941) is left untagged. "باز خواهد گمارد" is H7725, and H5414 moves to "خواهی نهاد".
- Genesis 40:14: on "بر من", the tag H5978 goes on بر and the suffix من stays untagged. אִתְּךָ and נָא are not rendered.
- Genesis 40:15: مرا (p15) is tagged H853 because אֹתִי has את and را is fused into the word. براستی renders the infinitive absolute (H1589).
- Genesis 40:16: وی is tagged H3130 because it stands in for the name Joseph.
- Genesis 40:19: تَنَت and تن ("your body") render מֵעָלֶיךָ and are tagged H5921. This is uncertain.
- Genesis 40:20: the second فرعون (p11) repeats the subject and is not in the Hebrew, so it is untagged.
- Genesis 40:23: را is untagged because the object is a verb suffix with no את. بلکه is tagged Hc.
- I took no numbers from neighbouring verses.
