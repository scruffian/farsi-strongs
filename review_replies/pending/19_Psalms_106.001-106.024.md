- Psalms 106:7 p25–p26 / p29: "در کنار" taken as עַל (H5921) and "نزد" as Hb of בְּיַם; the Persian ordering makes the mapping slightly uncertain.
- Psalms 106:9 p12–p13: grouped "از میان" as Hb (בַּתְּהֹמוֹת "through the depths").
- Psalms 106:18: "زبانه کشید" taken as וַתִּבְעַר (H1197), "شعله‌ها" as H3852, "سوزانید" as H3857; Persian و has no Hebrew vav behind it.
- Psalms 106:22 p7–p9: "به عمل آورده بود" is implied from עֹשֶׂה in 106:21 (ellipsis, not a verse-division difference), so left untagged.
- Psalms 106:23 p20: split "ایشان" (added, no suffix on מֵהַשְׁחִית) out of "هلاک کردن ایشان".
