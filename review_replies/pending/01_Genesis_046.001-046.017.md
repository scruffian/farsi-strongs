- Genesis 46:1 p6 داشت: tagged Hl, since it renders ל/וֹ ("what was his"). Fairly confident, but the possessive construction could also be left untagged.
- Genesis 46:1 p9 به: untagged. It renders the directional ה, which has no number.
- Genesis 46:1: split "قربانیها تقدیم" and grouped "تقدیم کرد" (H2076). قربانیها is H2077.
- Genesis 46:3 p19 در: untagged. It belongs with آنجا (שָׁם), not with the Hl of לְגוֹי.
- Genesis 46:4 p14 به‌یقین: H5927, rendering the infinitive absolute עָלֹה. The finite verb "باز خواهم آورد" is grouped as H5927.
- Genesis 46:5 p22 آوردن: H5375 (לָשֵׂאת). p23 او is left untagged because it renders the suffix of אֹתוֹ. It is unsure whether H853 should go on it.
- Genesis 46:8 p10 که: tagged Hd, with the article on הַבָּאִים acting as a relative. Unsure. p11 به: untagged because it is directional ה.
- Genesis 46:12 p30 بودند: H1961 (וַיִּהְיוּ).
- Genesis 46:15 p7 را: untagged, because it marks آنها and not Dinah. p8 با: Hc (וְאֵת דִּינָה "and Dinah"). p23 یعقوب: untagged, because it renders the suffix of בָּנָיו/בְנוֹתָיו. Unsure. p25/p27: split H7970 and H7969. p28 تن: H5315 (נֶפֶשׁ).
- Genesis 46:17 p4/p6: removed the extra numbers, so they are now יִמְנָה = H3232 and יִשְׁוָה = H3438.
